# Shared vs private

Team filing rule. Re-sort anything already in the wrong place.

## SHARED — true about me and the company

Put it here if another bot would be wrong without it:

- Who I am, voice, hard nos
- Customers, offers, pricing already decided
- Tools of record and how we log in (never raw secrets in chat — point at the vault)
- Standing rules (`always X, never Y`)
- DECISIONS log
- Named skills the whole company may use
- Role map: which bot owns which job

## PRIVATE — one bot, one task

- Notes while drafting a single email, post, or report
- Intermediate research that has not been promoted
- Scratch names, outlines, failed attempts
- Temporary credentials or one-off IDs (prefer not to store; delete after the task)

## Promotion

A private note becomes shared only when:

1. It is still true next week, and
2. A second bot would need it, and
3. The founder says yes (or the weekly prune marks it “promote”).

## Demotion / delete

If two bots disagree, do not average them. Audit, pick one, delete the other, write a rule so the loser cannot resurface.
