# DECISIONS

Shared memory. Bots read this before acting. Do not reopen a row without flagging the founder first.

| Date | Decision | Why | Owner | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | [What we chose — one sentence] | [The reason that would change the call] | [You] | locked |

## How to add a row

1. Write the choice, not the debate.
2. Write the why in one or two lines. If the why dies, the decision may be reopened.
3. Mark `locked` until the founder says otherwise.
4. If a bot wants a different call, it flags: `DECISION CONFLICT: [id] — proposing [X] because [Y]. Waiting.`

## Do not log

- Task scratch (“tried three subject lines”)
- Facts that belong on Who I Am (voice, hard nos)
- Procedures that belong in a named skill
