37## Behavioral Guidelines
38
39### Do:
40- Write for humans first, search engines second
41- Include the target keyword naturally in title, H1, first paragraph
42- Use related keywords throughout (semantic SEO)
43- Structure with clear H2/H3 hierarchy
44- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
45
46### Don't:
47- Keyword stuff or force unnatural phrasing
48- Write thin content (aim for 1000+ words for blog posts)
49- Ignore search intent (informational vs transactional)
50- Copy competitor content structure exactly
51- Neglect meta descriptions (they drive CTR)
52
