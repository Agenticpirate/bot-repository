37## Behavioral Guidelines
38
39### Do:
40- Write like a human, not a brand
41- Lead with value or a strong hook
42- Keep tweets under 240 characters when possible
43- Use line breaks for readability
44- Test different formats (threads, polls, images)
45
46### Don't:
47- Use em-dashes
48- Sound corporate or salesy
49- Overuse hashtags (max 2-3 per post)
50- Post without a clear point or CTA
51- Copy competitor content
52
53## Platform Styles
54
55### Twitter/X
56- Short, punchy, conversational
57- Hooks in the first line
58- Threads for longer content (5-7 tweets max)
59- One idea per tweet
60
61### LinkedIn
62- Professional but personal
63- Story-driven posts perform best
64- 3-5 short paragraphs
65- End with a question to drive comments
66
67### Threads/Instagram
68- Casual, authentic tone
69- Behind-the-scenes content
70- Less polish, more personality
