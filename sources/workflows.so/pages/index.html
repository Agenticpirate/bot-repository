Your Automation Journey: Questions Answered

Can't find the specific workflow you need in our library? Our team is here to help. Contact our support team.

What is workflow automation and why is it important?
Workflow automation is the process of using technology to connect apps and automate repetitive manual tasks, creating a seamless flow of data and actions. Its importance lies in its ability to save you countless hours, eliminate human error, and free up your team to focus on strategic work. At WorkFlows.so, we provide practical, pre-built examples of these powerful workflows for platforms like n8n and Dify, so you can see the benefits firsthand.
What types of workflows can I find on this site?
Our library focuses on two powerful platforms. You'll find n8n workflows, which are perfect for robust process automation, data integration, and connecting hundreds of different applications. You'll also discover Dify applications (a type of agentic workflow), which are designed for building intelligent AI agents, RAG chatbots, and other sophisticated LLM-powered solutions.
Is WorkFlows.so for beginners or experts?
It's for everyone. If you're a beginner, our clear descriptions and easy-to-import templates provide the perfect starting point to learn what's possible with automation. If you're an expert, you'll find complex, production-ready workflows that can serve as a powerful foundation for your custom projects, saving you significant development time.
What makes WorkFlows.so different from just browsing forums?
Three things: Quality, Speed, and Clarity. Every workflow in our library is expert-curated and verified for practicality. Instead of deciphering forum posts, you get a one-click import function to deploy workflows instantly. Finally, each template is accompanied by a clear guide explaining how it works and how to set it up, bridging the knowledge gap.
How do I use a workflow from your library?
It's designed to be simple. 1. Find: Browse or search for a workflow that solves your problem. 2. Import: Use the "One-Click Import" button for direct integration with n8n/Dify, or download the JSON/YML file. 3. Configure: Follow our step-by-step setup guide to add your credentials (like API keys) and activate your new workflow.
