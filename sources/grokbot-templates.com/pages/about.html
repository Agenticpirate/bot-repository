About the Grok Bot Templates directory

A public directory of Grok Bot templates, team blueprints, and use cases — maintained by the grokbot-templates.com team.

Last updated: 2026-09-01

What this is

Grok Bot Templates is a public directory of Grok Bot templates. Browse the wall, then install a bot by opening its x.ai share link. We list names, short descriptions, categories, and the original share URL — not prompt bodies.

Teams and use cases

The same catalog also covers Teams blueprints (a whole desk of bots that work together) and Use cases (cited setups with published prompts). Start from the homepage, or jump to those sections in the footer.

Where the catalog comes from

The source of truth lives on GitHub: awesome-grokbot-templates.

Who maintains this

This directory is maintained by the grokbot-templates.com team. Find us on X at @manonglianai.
