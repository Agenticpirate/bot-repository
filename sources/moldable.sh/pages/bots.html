Give the conversation somewhere to go.

A useful bot can talk, remember the shape of its role, reach the web and services connected to the workspace, and keep the work organized across devices.

Chat with a role

Open a direct lane or bring a bot into a channel. It carries standing instructions instead of making you restate the job every time.

Let it use the browser

Sign in once to a workspace-scoped browser on your Mac. The bot can navigate the sites its work requires.

Choose its apps

Select which installed apps the role can use, from Calendar and Tasks to software you build yourself.

Continue in Voice

Talk to the same bot with the same channel context from Mac or iPhone. Voice does not start a separate relationship.
