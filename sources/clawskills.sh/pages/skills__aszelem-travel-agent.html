What This Skill Does

Finds, books, changes, and cancels flights by sending plain-text emails to BonBook's travel service on the user's behalf. BonBook connects directly to GDSs and airline systems, stores traveler documents and preferences, and returns booking options within seconds. Every financial transaction requires explicit human approval before completing.

Accesses inventory through GDSs and airline systems directly, surfacing flights not visible on consumer search engines, while handling changes and cancellations via email without requiring the user to navigate booking sites.

When to use it

  • Booking a last-minute business flight with a single message
  • Changing a departure time when travel plans shift
  • Canceling a trip within the 24-hour free cancellation window
  • Finding multi-city routes across a range of dates and preferences
  • Locating direct flights on a specific airline without searching manually
Example Workflow
Here's how your AI assistant might use this skill in practice.
INPUT
User asks: Book me a roundtrip from NYC to Chicago, leaving Thursday morning, returning Sunday evening on United
AGENT
  1. 1Sends email to book@bonbook.co with the roundtrip request, dates, and airline preference
  2. 2Receives five flight offer summaries from BonBook within 15-25 seconds
  3. 3Presents offers to the user and waits for selection
  4. 4Directs user to the checkout page to verify flight details and enter payment
  5. 5Confirms booking after user completes payment
OUTPUT
Booking confirmation email with reference number, itinerary, and receipt arrives in the user's inbox within 3 minutes
