What This Skill Does

AI management advisor that applies 16 mentor philosophies (Musk, Inamori, Ma, Dalio, etc.) to leadership decisions, team operations, and cross-cultural communication. Runs offline as a decision framework or connects to manageaibrain.com for automated check-ins, tracking, KPI monitoring, and bidirectional data sync.

Applies structured mentor decision matrices to management problems instead of giving generic leadership advice, and learns your decision patterns over time.

When to use it

  • Preparing culturally appropriate feedback for an international team member
  • Getting a morning briefing prioritized by your chosen management philosophy
  • Running a C-suite board simulation before making a strategic hire
  • Designing daily check-in questions based on mentor frameworks
  • Resolving a team conflict using structured resolution guidance
Example Workflow
Here's how your AI assistant might use this skill in practice.
INPUT
User asks: How do I give negative feedback to my Filipino employee about consistently missing deadlines?
AGENT
  1. 1Loads active mentor philosophy framework from config
  2. 2Reads Filipino culture pack communication rules
  3. 3Identifies conflict between mentor style and cultural norms, applies culture-override rule
  4. 4Generates step-by-step feedback approach with specific phrasing adapted to Filipino communication preferences
  5. 5Suggests follow-up check-in questions aligned with mentor style
OUTPUT
A culturally adapted feedback script that respects indirect communication norms, with private delivery guidance and mentor-aligned follow-up questions
