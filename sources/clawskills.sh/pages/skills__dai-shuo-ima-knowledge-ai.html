What This Skill Does

Knowledge-base skill providing strategic guidance on workflow design, model selection, parameter optimization, and visual consistency for AI-generated image, video, and audio content. Contains 12 reference modules covering topics from character design and brand identity to color theory and commercial advertising templates. Designed to be consulted before calling IMA Studio's content creation APIs.

Acts as a pre-execution consultant that helps pick optimal models, parameters, and workflows before spending credits on generation APIs.

When to use it

  • Planning a multi-step video production workflow before generating clips
  • Choosing the right AI model and parameters for a product poster
  • Maintaining character consistency across a series of generated images
  • Selecting culturally appropriate brand colors for a specific regional market
  • Reviewing generated designs against common pitfalls before final delivery
Example Workflow
Here's how your AI assistant might use this skill in practice.
INPUT
User asks: Help me create a 16:9 high-quality product poster
AGENT
  1. 1Read parameter-guide.md to identify aspect ratio support per model
  2. 2Read model-selection.md to compare quality and cost trade-offs
  3. 3Select Nano Banana Pro 4K as the best fit for high-quality 16:9 output
  4. 4Call ima-image-ai with the chosen model and optimized parameters
OUTPUT
High-quality 4K product poster generated with optimal model settings and correct aspect ratio
