What This Skill Does

An alias package that redirects to raon-os, an AI-powered startup companion built for Korean founders. Install raon-os to access the full feature set, including business plan evaluation, government funding matching, a RAG pipeline, and Kakao integration.

When to use it

  • Evaluating a business plan before pitching investors
  • Finding matching government grants for an early-stage Korean startup
  • Querying startup documents through a RAG pipeline
  • Integrating startup workflows with Kakao
  • Checking funding eligibility for Korean government programs
