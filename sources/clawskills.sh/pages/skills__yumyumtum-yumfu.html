What This Skill Does

A multiplayer text RPG engine spanning 10 themed worlds, from Jin Yong wuxia novels to Harry Potter, Warrior Cats, Lord of the Rings, and Game of Thrones. Players each get a persistent character in a shared world with AI-generated art every scene, voice narration, PvP combat, and team quests. All interaction is natural language -- no commands needed after starting.

Combines persistent multiplayer RPG mechanics, AI art generation, and natural language interaction across multiple fictional universes without requiring game commands or technical knowledge to play.

When to use it

  • Running a group RPG session with friends in a Telegram chat
  • Playing through a branching wuxia adventure in 笑傲江湖 or 倚天屠龙记
  • Exploring Hogwarts as a student across seven school years with exams and Quidditch
  • Competing with friends in PvP martial arts duels with dice-based combat
  • Generating an illustrated HTML storybook of a completed adventure
