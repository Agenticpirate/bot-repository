What This Skill Does

Gift Genius finds Valentine's Day gifts by routing users to local florists and gift merchants based on location. It covers 2,099 products across flowers, chocolates, jewelry, candles, fragrances, and grooming from 10 merchants across the US, Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, and Europe. Returns 2-3 curated options with recipient matching, color preferences, and delivery timing.

Returns 2-3 matched picks with recipient-specific filters and direct checkout links instead of presenting hundreds of results to scroll through.

When to use it

  • Finding flowers for a wife with a $150 budget
  • Sending same-day Valentine's flowers before the noon delivery cutoff
  • Ordering flowers for a partner in Singapore from a different country
  • Choosing between jewelry and chocolates as a combo gift for a girlfriend
  • Picking orchids for a mom who wants something that lasts longer than a week
Example Workflow
Here's how your AI assistant might use this skill in practice.
INPUT
User asks: I need flowers for my wife, around $120
AGENT
  1. 1Detect user location and select the regional merchant (UrbanStems for US)
  2. 2Query the AirShelf API with recipient-matched terms and the stated budget range
  3. 3Filter results using the ideal_for field targeting wife or long-term partner
  4. 4Format 2 options with colors, vibe, longevity, and same-day delivery availability
  5. 5Offer a direct checkout link and ask if the user wants to proceed
OUTPUT
Two flower recommendations with product links, decision pack details, and same-day delivery confirmation
