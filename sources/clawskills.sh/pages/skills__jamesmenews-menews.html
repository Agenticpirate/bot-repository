What This Skill Does

Aggregates AI industry news from ME News APIs and delivers morning digests, evening summaries, 24-hour hot topic rankings, or curated selections. Content is scored by AI, deduplicated, and categorized into areas like large models, agents, funding, security, applications, and open source.

Combines multiple AI news feeds into a single scored and categorized digest instead of manually checking separate sources.

When to use it

  • Getting a morning briefing on overnight AI developments
  • Checking the hottest AI news from the past 24 hours
  • Scheduling automated daily AI newsletter delivery via cron
  • Reviewing evening summaries of funding rounds and product launches
  • Pulling top-scored AI articles for a team update
Example Workflow
Here's how your AI assistant might use this skill in practice.
INPUT
User asks: 查询 24H 热点新闻榜
AGENT
  1. 1Reads sources.md to confirm data source URLs
  2. 2Calls ME News APIs via curl to fetch recent articles
  3. 3Extracts title, summary, link, publish time, and AI score from responses
  4. 4Filters to articles published within the last 24 hours and deduplicates by title
  5. 5Sorts by AI score descending and selects top 10 results
OUTPUT
A formatted hot news report with 10 ranked AI articles, each showing category tag, title, summary, score, and source link
