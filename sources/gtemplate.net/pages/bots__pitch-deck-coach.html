About Pitch Deck Coach

Pitch Deck Coach is a Grok Bot template for reviewing a fundraising deck from an investor's likely point of view.

What it does

The live x.ai listing says the Bot reads a pitch deck and reports what an investor is likely to understand, believe, question, and remember. It then helps strengthen the story, supporting evidence, and individual slides.

Hiten Shah's launch post adds an important workflow detail: the Bot reads the whole deck first, summarizes the company and story it heard, and only then works through improvements. That can expose a gap between the story a founder intended and the one a reader actually receives.

Who it is for

The clearest fit is a founder preparing or revising a fundraising deck who wants a structured first critique before asking investors or advisers for time. It is not a substitute for feedback from the specific market, customer, or investor being targeted, and uploaded decks may contain confidential company information.
