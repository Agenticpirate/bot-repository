About Shorty

Shorty is a Grok Bot template for finding strong moments in proven long-form YouTube content and packaging them as captioned vertical Shorts.

What it does

The current x.ai listing describes a cost-conscious workflow that tries Opus Clip first, uses Descript as a backup, formats clips in 9:16, and produces weekday batches of roughly four to six. It creates and schedules content but is instructed not to delete it. Farzad's launch post says the Bot starts from the best-performing moments in the best-performing videos on his own channel.

That starting rule can narrow the search for hooks, but prior performance does not guarantee that a new Short will perform. Titles, captions, source rights, clip accuracy, and the final schedule still need review.

Who it is for

The clearest fit is a creator with an existing long-form library and enough performance history to decide what “proven” means. It is a weaker fit for a new channel with little source material or for someone clipping content they do not own.
