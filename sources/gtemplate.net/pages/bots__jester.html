About Memelord

Memelord is a Grok Bot template for generating captioned image and video memes from a prompt through the Memelord API.

What it does

The current x.ai listing says the Bot removes the need to hunt down a meme template by hand. Jason Levin's launch post originally introduced the shared template as Jester and described access to a large library of memes and videos. The stable catalog route remains /bots/jester, while the live x.ai listing now names the template Memelord.

The workflow can shorten meme assembly, but it does not guarantee that a joke is accurate, timely, original, or appropriate for its audience. Users still need to review the selected template, caption, source material, and final post.

Who it is for

The clearest fit is a creator or social team that already knows the joke it wants to make and needs a fast visual or short-video version. It is less useful when the task requires original illustration, detailed motion editing, or careful rights clearance.
