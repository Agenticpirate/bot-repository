About Home Robots

What it does, who it is for, who made it, and what early public replies reveal.

What it does

Home Robots is a Grok Bot template that lets one conversation control more than one household robot. Sawyer Merritt introduced it with support for a Navimow robotic mower and a Matic vacuum, while noting that other robots can be connected too.

The practical promise is to connect each device once, then ask one assistant to handle the lawn, floors, or another supported job instead of switching between manufacturer apps. Device support can change, so the x.ai listing remains the place to check before adding it.

Who it is for

The clearest fit is a household that already owns compatible devices and repeats routine commands across multiple robot apps. Someone with one unsupported device has less reason to add it, and any physical action still deserves clear confirmation and an easy stop control.

Created by Sawyer Merritt

Sawyer Merritt shared the template during the early rollout of Grok Bot sharing. His post names the initial Navimow and Matic connections and links directly to the x.ai Bot page. The interaction counts in the Hero are a dated snapshot, not install, retention, or satisfaction data.
