About Master (Orchestrator)

Master (Orchestrator) is a Grok Bot template for routing work across a team of specialist Bots without turning the coordinator into another worker.

What it does

The x.ai listing describes a lean orchestrator that does not keep recurring personal, advertising, website, or desk tasks for itself. It routes an incoming request to the specialist that should own it and confirms the handoff instead of silently assuming that another Bot picked it up.

Farzad introduced Master (Orchestrator) as the orchestration layer used for his media business. The public description does not expose the full specialist roster, evaluation rules, or recovery behavior, so the template should be treated as a starting structure rather than proof that every handoff will complete.

Who it is for

The clearest fit is someone who already has several task-specific Bots and wants one entrance for delegating work. A user with only one or two simple workflows may add more coordination than they need. Teams using the template should still make ownership, completion criteria, and risky actions explicit.
