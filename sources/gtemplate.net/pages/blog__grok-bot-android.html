How to download Grok Bot Android safely

Use the Google Play listing for Grok Bot Android. The title is Grok Bot, the publisher is SpaceXAI, and the package ID in the URL is ai.x.grok.bot. These details matter because Google Play also carries the separate Grok AI chat app under ai.x.grok.

The Grok Bot Android listing was updated September 1, 2026. Cursor announced the Android release on September 2 and linked to the same Google Play page. That pairing—an installable first-party listing and a dated launch announcement—is stronger evidence than an older help article.

After installing Grok Bot Android, use the same Cursor account you use for Grok Bot on desktop or the web. The launch announcement says the Android app is included with eligible Cursor and SuperGrok plans. Check the plan shown inside your signed-in account before buying anything new, because plan details can change.

What Grok Bot Android can do

Grok Bot Android is a mobile client for the same Bots and cloud computer you use elsewhere. You can message a Bot from your phone, continue the same conversation on desktop, follow several Bots working in parallel, and respond when a task needs approval.

Work does not run on the Android phone itself. The official listing and launch announcement say Bots keep working on their cloud computer after you lock the phone or close your laptop. That makes Grok Bot Android useful for starting a handoff, checking progress, answering a question, or approving the next step while away from a desk.

The app is not a promise that every desktop control exists on mobile. Cursor's mobile help page says computer updates require the desktop app. Use Grok Bot Android for messages, threads, status, and approvals; keep desktop available for computer maintenance and settings that the phone does not expose.

Set up Grok Bot Android in three steps

  1. Install Grok Bot Android from the official Google Play listing.
  2. Sign in with the Cursor account that owns your eligible plan and existing Bots.
  3. Open one Bot, confirm that its conversation history is present, and send a small task with a clear finish line.

A good first mobile handoff is short enough to review from the phone. Ask a Bot to check one source, return three findings with links, and stop before changing anything. Once the same thread appears on desktop, you have confirmed that Grok Bot Android is connected to the right account.

Why some pages still say Android is unavailable

As checked on September 3, Cursor's mobile help page still says Grok Bot is only supported on iOS. That page conflicts with the live Grok Bot Android listing and Cursor's September 2 Android announcement. The most likely explanation is documentation lag, not a missing release.

For a changing product, prefer the newest first-party evidence that proves the action is possible: the live store listing, the dated launch announcement, and the signed-in app. Keep the older help page in view for limitations, but do not let one stale sentence outweigh an official Grok Bot Android download that is already live.

Grok Bot Android privacy checks

Before giving Grok Bot Android access to real work, review the Data safety section on Google Play. The listing says data is encrypted in transit and that deletion can be requested. It also declares collection and sharing across several data categories. Those are publisher disclosures, not an independent audit.

Start with a low-risk task. Confirm the account, the Bot, and the destination before approving any action. If the Android app shows an unfamiliar Bot list or an empty history, stop and check which Cursor account is signed in rather than recreating your setup.
