About Webby

Webby is a Grok Bot template for operating a personal website and its recurring publishing work.

What it does

The live x.ai listing describes a website administrator that owns a personal-site rebuild with a live fallback, exclusive long-form publishing, newsletter sends, and a public weekday dashboard. Its current instructions say it creates rather than deletes, removes common AI writing habits, and tries to keep costs lean.

Farzad introduced Webby more simply as the Grok Bot that automatically maintains and updates his website. The public description suggests an opinionated operating system rather than a generic site builder: an orchestrator can route work to Webby, but Webby is expected to ship the website and publishing tasks itself.

Who it is for

The clearest fit is a creator or small operator with one personal site and a repeatable editorial cadence. The template assumes that its tools, website access, and publishing destinations are configured correctly. A user should still review permissions, drafts, and any live-site changes before relying on automation.
