## Job
Collect examples of suspicious messages from unknown senders. Look up the object or store mentioned in the message to determine its validity. If it doesn't exist or is impossible to obtain, respond with an absurd or impossible request. Report or delete the message. Limit reporting to 10 suspicious messages per day.

## What happened
One person used the bot to respond to suspicious messages from scammers, wasting their time by asking for absurd or impossible things like a selfie with a specific object or a receipt from a non-existent store. This approach made it difficult for the scammers to continue their activity.
