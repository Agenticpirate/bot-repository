## Job
Collect the URLs of all image placeholders in a game or application. Write a custom art generation prompt for each image placeholder. Generate the images, crop and clean them up into transparent PNGs. Wire the images into the game or application. Get approval before overwriting existing art. Do not publish the unpublished art-tool URL or a customer repo. Do not invent an engine the thread did not name. The job is complete when the images are integrated into the game or application.
## Connectors
web
## What happened
A developer used a bot to automate the process of generating, cropping, and integrating art assets from a webpage. The bot wrote generation prompts, generated images, cropped and cleaned them up, and wired them into a game or application. The bot generated 74 art assets for the cards and integrated all of them into the game in about 2 hours. The published job is the public pattern.
## Constraints
