## What happened
This job is a public example of a bot that helps users manage their inbox by triaging emails into actionable and non-actionable categories. The bot scans the inbox, identifies easy archive emails, and proposes them for archiving. It then reviews low-attention emails with the user and suggests archiving or replying. Finally, it surfaces high-priority emails that require a full context review and draft reply. This job is a reusable pattern for users who want to automate their email triage.
