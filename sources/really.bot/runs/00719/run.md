## What happened
This job was listed as a public Grok Bot prompt and copied onto really.bot for the directory. The user wanted to set up a bot that could cross-post their top-performing content across multiple platforms, while allowing for manual review and approval. The bot would search their posts from the last year, rank them by performance, select the top 10, adapt each for the destination platform, and schedule two approved posts per day across Threads, LinkedIn, and Bluesky over the next five days. The user also wanted to be able to review the selected posts and destination drafts with their schedule before publishing. This job is a public pattern for synchronizing content across multiple social media platforms.
