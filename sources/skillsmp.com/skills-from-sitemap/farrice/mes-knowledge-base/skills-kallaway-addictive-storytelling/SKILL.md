# Kallaway Addictive Storytelling — SKILL.md

## Domain
Neurochemical Retention Engineering — the science of keeping audiences locked in through prediction cycle architecture.

## Expert
Kallaway — Retention substrate between Content Psychology ("what to build") and Audience Obsession ("what to implant"). This domain answers: **"How do you keep them watching?"**

## Core Thesis
Dopamine is a **prediction chemical**, not a pleasure chemical. Content that lets the brain predict correctly produces zero engagement. Content that engineers prediction errors — surprise that is unexpected but immediately logical — produces neurochemical addiction. The Four-Step Addiction Loop (Stakes → Big Question → Head Fake → Rehook) is the operating system for this.

## Governing Metric
**Time-Per-Hand** — the gap between dopamine hits. Like a casino decreasing the time between bets, your content must decrease the gap between predictions. Shorter gaps = longer sessions.

## The Decision: Vending Machine vs. Slot Machine
Every piece of content is one of two things:
- **Vending Machine**: Outcome is predictable from setup → zero dopamine → viewer can leave at any time
- **Slot Machine**: Outcome is uncertain → sustained dopamine drip + periodic spikes → viewer is chemically locked in

All workflows in this domain convert vending machines into slot machines.

## Quality Rubric
All outputs from this domain are evaluated on:
1. **Prediction Coverage %** — at any given point, is there an active prediction? Target: ≥75%
2. **Time-Per-Hand Score** — average gap between dopamine hits vs. format benchmark
3. **The "Oh! not Huh?" Test** — every head fake produces surprise + understanding, never confusion
4. **Zero Vending Machine Sections** — no predictable sections in the final output
5. **Relay Race Transitions** — every section handoff maintains momentum (both runners at full sprint)
6. **Escalation Curve** — head fake surprise magnitude increases across cycles
