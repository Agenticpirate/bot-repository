EXHIBIT AThree things Spire doesn't doPG. 02 / 04

Negative spaceas a product spec.

Most analytics tools are defined by what they collect. Spire is defined by what it refuses to. No cookie. No fingerprint. No third-party domain. What you get back is the boring answer — pageviews, sessions, top pages, conversion — calculated cleanly, on your own infrastructure.

— PRINCIPLE 01No cookie. None.

Sessions are derived from a salted, daily-rotated hash of (IP + UA), which expires when the day does. No persistent identifier ever leaves the visitor's request. Your privacy banner can be deleted.

— PRINCIPLE 02No third-party domain.

The script is served from spire.yourdomain.com, the events go to the same place. Ad blockers don't block it because it isn't an ad-tech domain. There's no ad-tech domain.

— PRINCIPLE 03No per-user dossier.

Spire stores aggregate counts, not user paths. You can answer "how many visitors hit /pricing then /signup?" without ever storing a user record. The trade-off is honest; the privacy is real.

One forty-nine.
Once. On your subdomain.

Single static binary. SQLite by default; Postgres if you want it. One-line script tag, ten-line nginx block, dashboard at /_spire. Export to CSV/Parquet for whatever long-term warehouse you actually use.
