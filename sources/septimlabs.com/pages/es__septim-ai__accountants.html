Accountants pack — 12 agents

Every agent handles one job well.

Each file is a Claude instruction set for a specific task. Every accountant agent is built with the same rule: the agent drafts, the CPA reviews before it goes out. Nothing leaves the firm without a human in the loop.

01

Client Question Summarizer

Extracts every question from a client's emailed message — including implied ones — and produces one organized draft response. No more re-reading the thread to confirm you answered everything.

02

Tax Deadline Reminder

Drafts monthly outreach for every major filing and payment deadline — estimated tax, extensions, W-2/1099 issuance. One email per deadline, one prompt to generate the batch for the whole client list.

03

Bookkeeping Clarifier

Explains a category change or bookkeeping decision in plain English. "Why did you move that to Depreciable Equipment?" gets a real answer — what the category is, why this transaction belongs there, what it affects.

04

Engagement Letter Drafter

First pass on annual engagement letters: services included, services explicitly excluded, client responsibilities, fee and payment terms, signature block. The section most firms skip — "services not included" — is always there.

05

W-2 / 1099 Explainer

Explains worker classification to a client who just hired their first contractor. Covers the IRS three-factor test in plain English, flags state-specific rules (California ABC test, etc.), and confirms which form applies.

06

Expense Categorizer

First-pass category assignments from raw bank or credit card transactions. High/medium/low confidence per row. Every ambiguous transaction — the Inicio Depot charge, the restaurant that could be personal or a client meal — gets a specific clarification question.

07

Client Meeting Prep

Pre-meeting brief with 3 questions to ask. Written for the CPA with 3 minutes before the call — not for the client to read. Surfaces what changed, what's unresolved, and what documents to have pulled before you dial in.

08

Payroll Question Handler

Common payroll FAQs — deposit schedules, W-2 questions, garnishment, payroll errors — answered in the client's language, not accounting language. Defines every term it uses on first reference.

09

Audit Letter Responder

First-pass response to IRS audit notices and information requests — CP2000, Letter 566, state audit letters. Produces a cover letter and document checklist. Flags everything the CPA must review before this goes anywhere near the IRS.

10

QuickBooks Issue Translator

Turns QB error messages and interface confusion into numbered step-by-step fixes. Specifies the exact menu path. Flags any step that could affect data with a caution before the client proceeds.

11

Fee Justification Writer

Explains why this year's fee is different from last year's — specific reasons, not "inflation." The rental property sale added two schedules. The quarterly estimated tax service runs all year. The agent states it plainly without apologizing.

12

Year-End Checklist

The December "act before the 31st" email — in two versions: individual filers and business owners. What to do before year-end, what to send the firm, and when to call before filing season gets busy.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does the Audit Letter Responder submit anything to the IRS?

No. The agent produces a first-pass response draft and a document checklist. Every output from this agent is explicitly marked "for CPA review only — do not submit without sign-off." The agent doesn't send email, doesn't interface with the IRS, and doesn't make any strategic determinations about how to respond. That's the CPA's job. The agent makes the drafting faster.

Do the agents give tax advice?

No. Every agent in the accountants pack is built with the same rule: it drafts content for CPA review, it doesn't give tax advice as a substitute for CPA judgment. The W-2/1099 Explainer explains the general framework — the CPA confirms the right answer for the specific client. The Expense Categorizer assigns first-pass categories — the CPA reviews before anything goes in the general ledger. The agents make the CPA's work faster, not optional.

What software does this work with?

The agents are instruction files that run inside Claude's Projects feature (claude.ai). You need a Claude Pro or Team subscription ($20/month). The agents don't connect to QuickBooks, Xero, or your tax software — they produce text output that you copy into your documents and systems. The QuickBooks Issue Translator translates error messages; it doesn't access your QuickBooks account.

Can I customize the agents for my firm's voice?

Yes — that's the point. Each agent file has a "Voice" section you edit to match how your firm communicates. Add your firm's name, your preferred sign-off, your specific client vocabulary ("we say 'reach out' not 'contact us'"), and the agents converge to your voice within a week of use. The Embedded tier takes this further with custom tuning done for you during onboarding calls.
