· the problem ·

Anthropic sends an invoice. Not a digest.

You hit $300 in Claude Code spend last month. You know that because you got the invoice. You do not know which 3 sessions ate $180 of it, or whether this week is trending 40% higher than last week, or which model is quietly responsible for the spike.

· faq ·

Questions we already have answers to.

What data does Courier actually pull?
Two sources, your choice: (1) upload your Anthropic invoice PDF — we parse it in memory, extract model/date/cost per line-item, and discard the file. (2) paste the output of ccusage --json from your terminal — we read the structured JSON directly. We never ask for your Anthropic API key, account credentials, or payment details.
Is my data secure?
Raw PDF bytes are parsed inside the serverless function and never written to disk or a database. Only the extracted tuples (model, date, cost in USD) are stored — no filenames, no account IDs, no raw text. Supabase row-level security means your rows are only readable by your session. We store less than your bank does.
How do you parse my invoice PDF?
We use a text-extraction library (pdf-parse, MIT-licensed) against Anthropic's consistent invoice format: one line per model run with model name, date, token counts, and dollar amount. If the format changes or your invoice can't be parsed, we return an error and ask you to use the ccusage JSON path instead. We don't silently drop rows.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Monthly subscribers cancel anytime from the billing portal link in every digest email — no email to us required. Annual subscribers who cancel in the first 30 days get a full refund. After 30 days, the annual plan runs to its end date with no renewal. No dark patterns.
