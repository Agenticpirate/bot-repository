· rules ·

Short list. No fine print tricks.

  1. Commission of the invoice total, after Stripe fees and before sales tax, per the step-down tier above. One-time products only (all Septim products are one-time for now). If we ever launch a subscription we’ll write separate recurring terms.
  2. 90-day last-click attribution. UTM parameters on your link + Stripe session metadata. If two affiliates claim the same sale, the last click wins.
  3. FTC disclosure is required. Every piece of content containing your affiliate link must include a clear material-connection disclosure (e.g. #ad, #sponsored, "affiliate link", or equivalent plain-language statement) placed before the first link. This applies to blog posts, YouTube videos, tweets, newsletters, TikTok captions — everything. Violation is grounds for immediate termination and forfeit of unpaid commissions.
  4. No self-referrals, no incentivized clicks, no email spam. Organic content, paid ads (as long as they’re not bidding on "Septim" brand terms), newsletters, YouTube videos, blog reviews — all fine.
  5. No fake reviews or misleading claims. If you say Septim Flint catches every PR vulnerability, that’s false, and we’ll end the partnership. Honest positioning only.
  6. Refunded sales don’t pay commission. Our refund window is 30 days — payouts happen on day 31 for cleared revenue.
  7. Payouts in the currency of your choice. Stripe invoice by default. PayPal/wire/USDC on request. Minimum payout: $20 accrued.
  8. We can terminate with 7 days notice if rules 3, 4, or 5 are broken. Accrued commissions on cleared, non-refunded sales are still paid.
  9. Independent contractor relationship. You are not a Septim Labs employee, agent, or representative. You speak for yourself, not for the company. We don’t provide you an email address, employment benefits, or the authority to negotiate on our behalf.
