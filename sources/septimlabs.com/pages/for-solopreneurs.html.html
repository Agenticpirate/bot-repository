FAQ

Questions solopreneurs ask us.

I’m just one person — do I really need custom software?

If you’re paying for 3–5 different tools and still doing manual work to connect them, yes. Custom software isn’t about company size — it’s about whether your current tools actually fit how you work. A single tool that handles your specific workflow eliminates the subscription stack, the tab-switching, and the copy-paste busywork between apps. Time you save is revenue you earn.

Can’t I just use Notion/Airtable?

You can — until you can’t. Notion and Airtable are great general-purpose tools, but they break down when you need payment processing, automated client emails, scheduling with calendar sync, or a client-facing portal. You end up gluing together 5 tools with Zapier and still doing manual work. A custom build replaces the entire stack with one tool that does exactly what you need.

What’s the smallest project you’ll take?

Our Starter tier begins at $2,500. That covers a custom landing page with lead capture, a simple scheduling or booking tool, or a basic internal dashboard — all with $0/month hosting and mobile-responsive design. No project is too small if the problem is real.

What if I need changes after launch?

Two options. Pay-per-change: email us what you need, we quote it, you approve, we build it. No retainer, no commitment. Monthly retainer ($750/mo): covers ongoing updates and small feature additions with priority response. You also own the code — any developer can work on it.

Do I own the code?

100%. The codebase, the design, the deployment — it’s all yours. No vendor lock-in. Want to hire someone else to maintain it? Go for it. Want to sell your business and the tool goes with it? It does. Nobody can pull the rug.

What’s your tech stack?

Next.js, React, PostgreSQL (via Supabase), Stripe for payments, Twilio for SMS, Resend for email. Same stack the $50k agencies use — without the $50k price tag. Everything is modern, fast, and built to scale if your solo operation grows into a team.
