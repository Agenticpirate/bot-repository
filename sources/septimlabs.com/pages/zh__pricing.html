· questions ·

The ones we actually get.

Is there a subscription option?

No. Every product is one-time. Lifetime access. Updates delivered via the private repo you already have access to.

Can I upgrade a product I already bought?

Yes. Email us your Stripe receipt for the smaller product and we’ll apply the credit to a bundle. Drills $29 + $30 = Review Stack.

Is there an enterprise license?

Yes — for teams of 3+ seats, see /teams for progressive discounts (3 seats 15% off, 5 seats 25% off, 10 seats 27% off). Teams of 25+ are contact-for-pricing.

Do you offer PPP pricing for lower-income countries?

On request. Email us from a domain in your country; we offer 30-50% off for qualifying regions.

What payment methods work?

Stripe: all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, Cash App Pay. For wire or crypto, email us directly.

Is there a free tier?

Yes — 3 free Drills skills on GitHub, and Septim Forge (22 free dev utilities).
