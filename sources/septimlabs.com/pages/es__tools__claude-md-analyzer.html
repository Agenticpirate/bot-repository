How this works (and what it doesn’t do)

Client-side analysis only. Your CLAUDE.md is parsed in your browser. Nothing is sent to our servers. Open DevHerramientas → Network tab while running the analysis — you’ll see zero outbound requests after the initial page load.

What it checks:

  • Total length (flag if > 1500 lines — most is aspirational, not operational)
  • Ratio of instructions (imperative verbs) vs. aspirational language
  • Corporate-speak terms that dilute signal (“leverage”, “synergy”, “game-changing”, etc.)
  • Pattern detection for recurring requests that should be Drills (PR review, migration safety, test gaps, etc.)
  • Repeated phrases across sections (suggests a skill, not a freeform prompt)

What it doesn’t do: It’s not a full lint. It’s a tight set of heuristics that surface the 5 most common CLAUDE.md anti-patterns. If you want the actual enforcement — skills that refuse to do the wrong thing — that’s what Septim Drills is for.
