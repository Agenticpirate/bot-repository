Every seat gets the full delivery.

01

Private repo invite per seat

Every seat receives a GitHub invite to the private repo(s) their package covers. We use your provided GitHub usernames list — no admin-panel access required.

02

One invoice, any currency

Stripe invoice in USD by default. EUR/GBP/CAD on request. Wire, ACH, or international cards all accepted. Net 30 available at 10+ seats.

03

Lifetime access per seat

Every seat is a full license. No renewal, no seat-reallocation expiration. If a team member leaves, the license stays with them — they can’t re-use it at their next job under your org.

04

Updates for the whole team

Push to the private repo = every seat can pull. No versioning fragmentation, no "my teammate has a newer release" drift.

05

Submit the team quote form and you’ll have a draft Stripe invoice in your inbox within 2 business hours (weekdays, US Eastern). We’ll CC your billing contact if you list one.

Same refund window as individual licenses. Per-seat refund possible in the first 30 days.
