// first title

LAST EFFECTS — in production

You are the last appraiser at a dead man's estate sale. Everything he owned has a story. You buy it, sell it, study it, and slowly realize the collection is a confession.

Godot 4.x Idle / narrative hybrid Steam Early Access (target: Q3 2026) Solo + AI pipeline

What it is, tactically

An idle/incremental appraisal loop stacked on a narrative investigation frame. You run an estate sale: objects enter your shop, you appraise them, sell them, keep some. Each object comes with provenance — receipts, letters, ledger entries. The provenance is the puzzle. The story emerges object by object.

The idle mechanics are real: production pipeline, prestige resets, meta-progression. The narrative mechanics are real: each case has a hidden solution, each reset reveals another layer. Neither is a skin on the other; they interlock.

Steam capsule, screenshots, and wishlist page coming in stages as the build progresses. Community updates weekly after the DEED launch below.

// about the studio

How we operate

Septim Studios is the games division of Septim Labs. Labs ships developer tools (Forge, Drills, Vault, Agents Pack, Product Kit, and more). Studios ships games.

The company is small. The operating model is an AI-augmented solo team: one human founder plus a defined agent roster (Atlas, Canon, Nova, Tally, Ward, Juno, Pip for cross-division; Quill, Vale, Kael, Solen, Kite, Bram, Wren, Silt, Tack, Halo, Fern, Spire, Floe for Studios-specific roles). Every game is built end-to-end in this configuration.

We don't raise. We ship. Labs revenue funds Studios. Studios revenue is Steam-first, with the DEED token as community-signal layer, not as a funding mechanism.
