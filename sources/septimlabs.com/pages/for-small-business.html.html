FAQ

Questions small businesses ask us.

Is custom software only for big companies?

Not anymore. Modern frameworks and free-tier hosting have dropped the cost of custom software by 60–70%. A small business CRM that would have cost $40,000+ five years ago now starts at $2,500–$6,000 — less than what many businesses spend on SaaS subscriptions over the same period. If you have 1–50 employees and you’re paying monthly for tools that don’t quite fit, custom software is now within reach.

Can I really get $0/month hosting?

Yes. Platforms like Vercel and Netlify offer generous free tiers that most small business apps never outgrow. No credit card required, no hidden fees. We configure this as part of every build. If your app eventually outgrows the free tier, paid plans start at $20/month — not $200.

What if I only have a $5k budget?

Our Starter tier ($2,500–$5,000) covers landing pages, lead capture, scheduling tools, and simple internal tools — all with $0/month hosting and mobile-responsive design. Many small businesses start here and expand later as they grow. We scope every project individually and give you a fixed price upfront. No hourly billing surprises.

What if I need changes later?

Two options. Pay-per-change: email us what you need, we quote it, you approve, we build it. No retainer required. Monthly retainer ($750/mo): covers ongoing updates, bug fixes, and small feature additions with priority response. Either way, you own the code — you can also hire any developer to maintain it.

How long does it take?

Starter projects: 1–2 weeks. Full CRM or internal tool: 4–8 weeks. We show you progress every two weeks during the build, and you get a 60-day satisfaction guarantee on the finished product.

Do I own the code?

100%. The codebase, the design, the deployment — it’s all yours. No vendor lock-in. Want to hire another developer later? Go for it. Want to sell the business and the software goes with it? It does.
