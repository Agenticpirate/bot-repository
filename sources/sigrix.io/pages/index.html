About ai crews

What is an AI crew?

An AI crew is a team of AI agents that work together: a manager plans the job and delegates each step to a specialist member, so the crew completes a whole multi-step workflow instead of a single task.

Every AI crew on Sigrix is composed from building blocks you can see — a manager persona, member roles, and the prompts that drive them — and ships with a tested run so you know what it does before you buy.

Compare AI crews by the workflow they automate, the models and platforms they run on, the roles on the team, and price — then run the one you pick or adapt it to your own process.

People searching for an AI crew usually want a ready-made team of agents they can run on a whole workflow, with the roles and tested steps laid out before they buy. Popular searches: ai crew marketplace, multi-agent ai, ai agent team, buy ai crew, crewai marketplace. Related: ai agents, multi-agent systems, ai personas, ai automation, crewai.

What's inside an AI crew

01
A manager that delegates
A manager agent reads the goal, plans the steps, and hands each one to the right member — coordinating the team instead of doing every task itself.
02
Specialist members
Each member is an agent with a focused role — research, writing, review — driven by its own prompt, so the crew brings the right skill to each step.
03
Composed from building blocks
A crew is built from personas and prompts you can inspect. The listing shows the roles on the team and how they hand work off to each other.
04
Tested end-to-end run
Each crew ships with a captured run on a tested model, so you can see the whole workflow — and its token cost — before you buy.

Crews vs agents, assistants & prompts

A crew is one of several AI building blocks on Sigrix. Here is how it differs from the others:

Prompt
A single instruction for one task. A crew strings many roles and steps together and runs them as a team.
Agent
One autonomous tool that runs a task. A crew is several agents working together under a manager that delegates.
Assistant
Works with you turn by turn. A crew works for you — you set the goal and the team executes the whole workflow.
Crew
A team of agents — a manager plus specialist members — that plans, delegates, and completes a multi-step job together.
