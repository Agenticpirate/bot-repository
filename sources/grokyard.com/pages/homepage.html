How it works

Three ordinary steps.

1

A builder lists a config.

They add a public share link, describe personality, skills, and routines, and choose free showcase or a priced unlock (min $8).

2

You preview on Grokyard.

Browse the yard, filter by job, and read the public config before you open anything in Grok Bot.

3

Open in Grok Bot.

Free links open immediately. Priced ones unlock after checkout — then tap Add to Grok Bot in the app.

Free configs stay public. On paid sales Grokyard keeps a 15% platform fee — not added on top for the buyer.
