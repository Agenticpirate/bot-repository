About Grokyard

Grokyard is a public directory of Grok Bot templates — shareable Bots you can open on x.ai and add to your Grok Bot app.

What is a Bot template?

A Grok Bot template is a published Bot configuration — its skills, routines, prompts, and personality — shared via a public link. Creators publish in the Grok Bot app and get a URL. Recipients open that URL on x.ai, preview the Bot, and choose Add to Grok Bot.

There is no downloadable zip or JSON file for end users. Grokyard only lists those public share links.

What gets copied

  • ✓Bot configuration, skills, routines, and prompts
  • ✓Personality and job description as defined by the creator
  • ✗Creator's computer, files, or local environment
  • ✗Logins, API keys, or secrets
  • ✗Conversation history or private data

Third-party templates

Bots listed on Grokyard are third-party templates. They are not made by, endorsed by, or affiliated with xAI, SpaceXAI, or Cursor. When someone adds a shared Bot, they accept Grok Bot's third-party bot terms.

How sharing works

  1. 1. Publish your Bot in the Grok Bot app and get a public share link.
  2. 2. List that link here on Grokyard.
  3. 3. Visitors open the link on x.ai and choose Add to Grok Bot — the Grok Bot app is required to finish adding it.

Adding a shared Bot creates a copy of its config and skills. It does not give recipients your computer, logins, or conversation history.

List your Bot

Published your Bot in Grok Bot? List it on Grokyard as a free config showcase or a priced listing. Sign in, paste your share URL, and fill in the public config preview (personality, skills, routines).
