# MLA 9 Referencing

Build MLA 9th edition citations: a Works Cited list using the container system, parenthetical `(Author Page)` in-text citations with no comma or year, and headline capitalization.

## Steps

1. Read `references/mla-quickref.md`, then match the source type:

   - Book: `Author, A.A. Title of Book. Publisher, Year.`
   - Journal (container system): `Author, A.A. "Title of Article." Journal Name, vol. x, no. x, Year, pp. xx-xx.`
   - Web page: `Author, A.A. "Title of Page." Site Name, Day Month Year, URL.`

2. In-text: parenthetical `(Smith 12)`, no comma, no year, page number only when the source has pages. Narrative: `Smith (12) argues ...` Two authors: `Smith, John, and Jane Doe` in the list; `(Smith and Doe 12)` in text. Three or more: `(Smith et al. 12)` from the first citation.

3. Apply the MLA signatures:
   - Headline capitalization for titles (capitalize major words), not sentence case
   - Article/web titles in quotation marks; container titles (journals, sites, books) in italics
   - DOI/URL written without `https://` (MLA 9 convention): `doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx`
   - Access dates discouraged; include only when the source has no publication date and the instructor requires it
   - Date format: Day Month Year (12 March 2024)

4. The container system: a source can live in a container (journal, edited volume, website, database). After the source title comes the container title, other contributors, version, number, publisher, publication date, location (pages/URL). Second containers (a chapter in an anthology republished on a database) repeat the pattern after the first container's location.

5. Works Cited: alphabetical by lead author surname, hanging indent. No separate bibliography; sources consulted but not cited stay out.

6. Verify: every in-text citation matches a Works Cited entry by author name, and every entry is cited at least once in the text.

## Pitfalls

- MLA never uses a comma between author and page: `(Smith 12)`, never `(Smith, 12)`.
- No years in in-text citations; the year lives only in the Works Cited entry.
- URLs without `https://`; drop `http://www.` where it reads clean.
- Missing authors start the entry with the title; alphabetize by the first word ignoring articles.
