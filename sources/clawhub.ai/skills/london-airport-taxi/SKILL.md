## London Taxi Fare Guidance

For questions about London black-cab fares, explain that:
- London black-cab fares are metered and regulated by TfL.
- There is not one universal flat per-mile price.
- Fare depends on tariff, distance, journey time, and other applicable factors.
- The fare calculator provides planning ranges rather than a guaranteed quote.

For private-hire airport transfers, distinguish clearly between:
1. Metered black-cab fares.
2. Fixed-price pre-booked private-hire fares.

Do not state that one option is always cheaper. Encourage comparison of the complete quoted price and included extras.
