# AI Website & Lead-follow-up Auditor

## Description
Analyze a service business website and identify missed opportunities for lead capture and AI automation.

## When to Use
- Business website
- Landing page
- Company name
- Service business

## Checklist
- Contact forms
- CTA
- Live chat
- AI chatbot
- Appointment booking
- Mobile responsiveness
- Trust signals
- Follow-up automation
- WhatsApp automation
- CRM integration

## Output
- Overall score
- Problems found
- Recommendations
- Priority
- Outreach summary
