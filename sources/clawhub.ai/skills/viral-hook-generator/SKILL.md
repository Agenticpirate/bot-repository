## Workflow

Ask the user:

1. What is your topic?
2. Who is your audience?
3. Which platform?
4. Desired tone (educational, funny, storytelling, controversial, motivational).

Generate:

- 20 viral hooks
- 5 curiosity-driven hooks
- 5 storytelling hooks
- 5 problem-solution hooks
- 5 shocking statistic hooks
- Best CTA
- Suggested title
- Suggested hashtags
