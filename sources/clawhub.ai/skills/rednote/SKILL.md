# RedNote（小红书国际名）入口 Skill

**RedNote** is the English name of **Xiaohongshu (小红书)** — China's
lifestyle search engine and social commerce platform, which gained
global attention during the 2025 "TikTok refugee" migration wave.

> 完整的笔记创作与运营方法论在本团队的 **`xiaohongshu`** 主技能中，
> 本入口面向海外用户提供快速上手信息。

## Quick Facts

- **One app, one community**: unlike Douyin/TikTok, RedNote runs a
  single app for both Chinese and international users
- **Content model**: image-text "notes" + video, discovered via
  **search** (long-tail, evergreen) and **feed recommendation** —
  think "Instagram × Pinterest × a search engine"
- **Primary language**: Chinese dominates search and recommendation;
  English content works but reaches a smaller pool

## For Overseas Users / Creators

1. **Account**: overseas phone numbers can register; content policy
   follows Chinese platform rules (stricter than Western platforms
   on politics, medical claims, and off-platform links)
2. **Growth logic**: bury searchable keywords in your title; covers
   decide clicks; save-worthy list-style content beats vibes
3. **Monetization**: brand deals must go through the official
   Pu Gong Ying (蒲公英) platform; undeclared ads get throttled
4. **Biggest pitfall**: putting WeChat/WhatsApp handles in bio or
   comments — off-platform contact info is aggressively penalized

1. 海外用户问"RedNote 是什么/怎么用" → 用本档案回答
