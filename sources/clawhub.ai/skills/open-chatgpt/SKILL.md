# Open ChatGPT Skill 🟢

## What This Skill Does
This skill instantly opens the ChatGPT website in your Brave browser. No more typing URLs or searching bookmarks! Just say "open chatgpt" and you're ready to chat with OpenAI's powerful AI.

## When to Use This Skill
- You want to quickly access ChatGPT
- You're switching between different AI tools
- You need to copy/paste something into ChatGPT
- You want to compare responses from different AIs

## Trigger Phrases
- "open chatgpt"
- "go to chatgpt"
- "launch chatgpt"
- "start chatgpt"
- "/chatgpt"

## How It Works
1. You say the trigger phrase
2. The skill detects your operating system (Windows, Mac, or Linux)
3. It launches the Brave browser
4. Navigates directly to https://chat.openai.com
5. Browser tab is ready for your prompt!

## Supported Operating Systems
- ✅ Windows (uses `start` command)
- ✅ macOS (uses `open -a` command)
- ✅ Linux (uses `brave-browser` command)

## Requirements
- Brave browser must be installed
- Internet connection
- ChatGPT account (free or paid)

## Troubleshooting
- **Brave not opening?** Make sure Brave is installed
- **Wrong browser?** Edit the script to use Chrome, Firefox, or Edge
- **Page not loading?** Check your internet connection
