# Singapore Family Trip Plan (1 Week with 3 Kids)

## Day 1: Arrival and Acclimatization
- Arrival at Changi Airport
- Check-in at family-friendly hotel (e.g., Resorts World Sentosa)
- Relax and unpack
- Dinner at hotel restaurant

## Day 2: Universal Studios Singapore
- Full day at Universal Studios Singapore
- Enjoy rides, shows, and family-friendly attractions
- Dinner at Universal's theme park restaurant

## Day 3: Sentosa Island
- Morning: S.E.A. Aquarium and Madame Tussauds
- Afternoon: Universal Studios Singapore (if time permits)
- Evening: Night Safari or Universal's Singapore ride
- Dinner at Sentosa restaurant

## Day 4: Gardens by the Bay and Marina Bay Sands
- Visit Supertree Grove and Flower Dome
- Explore Marina Bay Sands and its attractions
- Dinner at Marina Bay Sands restaurant

## Day 5: Clarke Quay and Chinatown
- Stroll through Clarke Quay and enjoy dinner at a riverside restaurant
- Explore Chinatown and its cultural sites
- Evening: Night walk along the river

## Day 6: Pulau Ubin and Food Tour
- Morning: Take a ferry to Pulau Ubin
- Explore the island's nature trails and cultural sites
- Lunch at a local eatery on the island
- Afternoon: Food tour in Kampong Glam or Little India
- Dinner at a local food market

## Day 7: Departure
- Morning: Free time for last-minute shopping or relaxation
- Check-out from hotel
- Departure from Changi Airport

## Tips for Families with Kids
- Book attractions with kid-friendly options and family passes
- Plan for rest days to avoid fatigue
- Use public transport or pre-arranged shuttles for convenience
- Pack snacks and essentials for long days out
- Consider guided tours for ease and safety

## Budget Considerations
- Accommodation: $150-$300 per night for family-friendly hotels
- Attractions: $50-$100 per child for entry fees and tours
- Food: $30-$50 per day for meals and snacks
- Transportation: $50-$100 for ferry tickets and shuttles

## Additional Notes
- Check for any school or work commitments during the trip
- Ensure all necessary travel documents are in order
- Consider travel insurance for added peace of mind
- Plan for flexible schedules to accommodate unexpected delays or changes
