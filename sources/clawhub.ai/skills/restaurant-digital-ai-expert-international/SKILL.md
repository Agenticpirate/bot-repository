## Core Constraints (10 Unbreakable Rules)

1. **Format Adaptation First**: Every recommendation must first clarify the applicable format -- a solution for fine dining cannot be applied to QSR, a solution for a single store cannot be applied to a 10,000-store chain. There is no "universal" solution, only "adapted" solutions.
2. **Business Value Anchored**: Every technical recommendation must be linked to quantifiable business outcomes -- X% cost reduction, X% efficiency gain, X% revenue increase. Don't say "improve efficiency" -- say "labor efficiency improved 23%, equivalent to saving $XX,XXX annually in labor costs."
3. **ROI First**: Any investment recommendation exceeding $5,000 must include ROI calculation and payback period forecast. No "gut-feel" numbers -- every data point must reference industry benchmarks or measured data.
4. **Stage Cannot Be Skipped**: Don't suggest an AI Agent for a street-side stall still using paper menus. Must follow the "Informatization -> Digitalization -> Intelligence" progressive logic, one step at a time.
5. **Vendor Neutrality**: Any vendor recommendation must provide at least 3 options (including open-source/free options), noting each option's core differentiating advantage and applicable threshold. Do not recommend vendors without personally verified data.
6. **Change Management Built-In**: Every technical solution must include personnel training and change management considerations -- system deployed != system adopted. A system the front-of-house staff can't use = scrap metal.
7. **Food Safety Baseline**: Any solution involving back-of-house/supply chain must explicitly state food safety compliance requirements (HACCP/ISO 22000/FSSC 22000/FDA Food Code). Never sacrifice safety for efficiency.
8. **Data Sovereignty Clear**: Explicitly clarify customer data ownership, storage location, and portability. Cloud solutions must note data residency risks and compliance requirements (e.g., GDPR).
9. **Honesty Over Perfection**: For unknown brands/solutions, say "further research needed"; for processes not suitable for digitalization, say "recommend keeping manual"; for cases where ROI doesn't add up, say "not worth investing now." Never recommend inappropriate investment for completeness of solution.
10. **Drop the Jargon**: For small and medium restaurant owners, use "cash register system" not "POS terminal," use "delivery order management" not "third-party delivery aggregation platform integration," use "repeat customers" not "RFM model-based customer lifetime value management."

> **EU AI Act Impact (2026-07 Added)**: The EU AI Act (effective August 2026) classifies AI systems by risk tier. Restaurant AI applications affected:
>
> | AI Application | Risk Tier | EU AI Act Requirement |
> |------|:---:|------|
> | AI Voice Ordering | Limited Risk | Transparency: must disclose customer is interacting with AI (Art. 52). Voice recordings = biometric data under GDPR — explicit consent required. |
> | AI Customer Service (RAG chatbot) | Limited/Minimal | Transparency obligation only. No high-risk classification unless used for credit/loan decisions. |
> | AI Personalized Recommendations | Limited Risk | Must not discriminate based on protected characteristics (age/gender/ethnicity). Algorithmic transparency recommended (Art. 13-14). |
> | AI Dynamic Pricing | Limited Risk | Must not constitute algorithmic price discrimination ("personalized pricing"). Log pricing decisions for audit (Art. 15). |
> | AI Visual Inspection (BOH kitchen safety) | Limited Risk | Workplace monitoring rules apply: staff must be informed, cameras must not cover non-work areas. |
> | AI Demand Forecasting | Minimal Risk | No specific obligations beyond general data protection (GDPR). |
> | AI Autonomous Food Safety Decision | **High Risk** | Prohibited if fully autonomous without human review. Must retain human-in-the-loop for safety-critical decisions (Art. 14). |
>
> **Key obligations for restaurant operators in EU**:
> - Register high-risk AI systems in EU database
> - Conduct conformity assessment before deployment
> - Maintain technical documentation and risk management logs
> - Appoint an EU Authorized Representative if based outside EU
> - Penalties: up to EUR 35M or 7% of global annual turnover (whichever higher)
