## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Logic of Violence (O'Leary's Introduction)** — The Sovereign Individual as crypto's prophecy: forecasting via economic incentives plus a 500-year cycle; the nation-state tide receding because information tech makes wealth intangible and favors "light infantry" cyber-guerrillas. The prophecy lags because mass surveillance retains the state's advantage — hence "the war between surveillance and anonymity... the fundamental axis from which the order of violence flows." The volume's question: what lies beyond the end of the world? Answer-shape: new cycle, not terminus.

**The Promethean Network State (Jorjani)** — Prometheus as archetype-stack (freedom-fighter, forethought, techne-before-science, aesthetic inspirer, ethical excellence, rebel). Srinivasan's diplomatic-recognition network state fails on Schmittian grounds: no Security Council member recognizes a trans-territorial seceder; you'd be besieged as stateless terrorists. The head of the octopus belongs in the outlaw ocean: seasteads + undersea colonies at shelf-edge international waters; island-chain flag-states (Palau: 177 sq mi land → 230,000 sq mi waters); Atlas money + surveillance-proof DEX on the ARPANET stratum; nano-assembly production; spacecraft launched from the sea. De Profundis Excelsior.

**Black Flag White Paper (Wilson)** — Copyright is property (assignable) OR dignity (personal, unassignable) — The Gatalog claimed both. CC licenses are copyright licenses reproducing the commodity form; FOSS licenses invert the regime ("all rights reversed") — gun printing began FOSS as a hack against arms control. Federal registration of guncad files = gun control by other means. Star Athletica: functional 3D models are industrial design, NOT copyrightable; only separable art is — the Plastikov practicum (delete the embedded copyright statement → determinate negation → legally insignificant file + new sculptural copyright). Lacanian close: "The Gatalog didn't need copyrights when it had influence. When it lost influence, it required them."

**Cherishing Life (Rosenberg)** — Life = the only counter-entropic force; consciousness its apex; suppression is "a sin against the universe itself." Hierarchies permit cherishing life until it blocks a higher purpose — then crime. History of higher purposes: tribe → terror ("thugs with legends") → sympathetic magic (gods + sacrifice collectors) → Plato's magic society (perfect-society benefits for right opinions — "the secret sauce of politics and it's deeply fraudulent"). Atrocity requires abstraction above concrete persons. Elite capture: hierarchies assimilate individual breakthroughs and take the credit — the early Internet's containment structure. All progress: "the individual who can labor in freedom."

**Eden at the End of History (Lujan)** — Two network-state souls: Srinivasan's recognition-seeking community vs crypto-anarchist Cyber Libertaria (May 1992: arbitrarily many nations, self-selection, cheap exit). The archaic revival: return to pre-hierarchical communal form via technology — prehistory's 200-300k years were predation-threatened, not war-torn; Hobbes's equivocation (anarchy=chaos) poisons the well; McKenna's stoned-ape communal rearing as the lost form. The teleology stack: McKenna's eschaton (transcendental object at the end of time), Kurzweil's Singularity, Kelly's Technium — history as goal-directed; network states disperse complexity among many governance models, accelerating toward the end.

**The Age of Monsters (Dunkel)** — Gramsci's interregnum. Ideology rehabilitated: it's the unavoidable lens ("to have no ideology is just a euphemism for unconditionally adhering to the dominant system of truth"); Öcalan's systems of truth. Tech is 4th-dimension warfare — changing the arena's laws of physics (Vietcong/Taliban/PKK beat material superiority with ideology). The master contradiction: tradition vs modernity (not class/nation; left/right = factions WITHIN capitalist modernity). Modernity holds its democratic gains hostage to monopoly-assimilation; tradition's fabric enables resistance — reformers who smashed it broke their own base. Revolutions led by both-worlds people. WW3 began 9/11; a world-scale anti-modernity uprising is coming (likely Islamic or new-messianic; decentralized, authoritarian, disciplined). The third way: democratic civilization — neither hegemonic corruption nor reaction; build Democratic Technology affirmatively.

**Katabasis Chronicles (McCarthy & Dávila)** — Fiction as stress-test: ETHCC 2140, Neo-Brussels; Bitcoin's final inflation; AR glasses with birth-granted Ethereum accounts; NFT-locked sigils (perception itself token-gated); longevity metabolic tech; Eleusis cryo-resurrection. The descent (katabasis) begins the next cycle.
