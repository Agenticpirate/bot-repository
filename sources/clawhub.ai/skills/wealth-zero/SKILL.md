## 2. Bootstrapping vs Funding

### Bootstrapping (Mohamed's path)
✅ **Advantages**:
- Keep 100% control
- No interest or investors
- Forced to be profitable
- Halal-compatible (no riba)

❌ **Disadvantages**:
- Slower growth
- Must carry risk yourself
- Limited capital

### When you MUST bootstrap:
- First time you build something
- When you have no track record
- When the product/service can start with 0-5,000 DKK
- When you want to keep control

### Mohamed's bootstrapping strategy:
1. **FactSage** — $0 production (free tools + server)
2. **Bots/Affiliate** — minimal hosting cost
3. **Trading** — use passive income to fuel
4. **FlowAxe** — build on own server, no hosting cost
5. **GravitonHost** — sell before you build (pre-sale)

## 3. Resource Allocation (Time, Money, Energy)

### The 3 Constraints:
| Resource | How to use it best |
|----------|--------------------|
| ⏱ **Time** | Only 24 hours/day. Spend time on what gives the MOST value |
| 💰 **Money** | 25$/mo budget. Spend on what gives the MOST ROI |
| ⚡ **Energy** | Most energy in the morning. Spend on the most important task |

### The 80/20 Principle (Pareto):
- 20% of effort gives 80% of results
- Find the 20% — do only that
- The rest: automate, delegate, or drop

### Mohamed's 80/20 analysis:
- **FactSage** ≈ 20% time → 80% passive income potential ✅
- **Trading** ≈ 80% time → 20% profit (until funded) ❌
- **FlowAxe/Axe** = 0% income until launched

**CONCLUSION**: FactSage should be the main focus until it generates stable profit

## 4. What Rich Entrepreneurs Do Differently

### The 7 Habits:
1. **One project at a time** — they don't spread themselves thin
2. **Revenue FIRST** — they build first, design afterwards
3. **They say NO** — to 90% of opportunities
4. **They automate** — systems > hard work
5. **They learn from failure** — fast failures, cheap lessons
6. **They surround themselves with skilled people** — agent team = smart move
7. **They think in years, not months** — patience

### Rich vs Poor Mindsets:
| Rich mindset | Poor mindset |
