### Core Techniques Covered

**1. Two-Digit × 11 Multiplication**
- Method: Split digits, middle is sum
- Example: 23 × 11 = 2 (2+3) 3 = 253
- Example: 45 × 11 = 4 (4+5) 5 = 495
- Special case: 56 × 11 = 5 (5+6) 6 = 5 11 6 → 616

**2. Three-Digit Square Calculation**
- Method: (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
- Example: 105² = (100+5)² = 10000 + 1000 + 25 = 11025
- Example: 98² = (100-2)² = 10000 - 400 + 4 = 9604

**3. Complement Subtraction Method**
- Transform subtraction into addition
- Example: 1000 - 387 = ?
- 387's complement to 1000 is 613
- So 1000 - 387 = 613

**4. Cross Multiplication (Any Two-Digit Numbers)**
- Formula: (10a+b)(10c+d) = 100ac + 10(ad+bc) + bd
- Example: 12 × 13
- Step 1: 1×1 = 1 (hundreds)
- Step 2: 1×3 + 2×1 = 5 (tens)
- Step 3: 2×3 = 6 (ones)
- Result: 156

**5. Finger Multiplication (6-10)**
- No memorization needed
- Visual finger counting method
- Perfect for children learning tables

**6. Universal Speed Formulas**
- Squaring numbers ending in 5
- Multiplying by 9, 99, 999
- Division shortcuts
