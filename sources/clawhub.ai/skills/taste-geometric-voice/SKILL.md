## Style Tokens

- `calligraphic rhythm as emotion` — script conveys feeling through proportion and flow
- `infinite geometric pattern (girih)` — patterns that suggest continuation beyond the frame
- `horror vacui as devotional fullness` — every filled surface is an offering of attention
- `gold on lapis contrast` — the most sacred color pairing in manuscript tradition
- `arabesque organic geometry` — plant forms abstracted into mathematical curves
- `mathematical precision as spiritual practice` — exactness as a form of devotion
- `bilateral symmetry with micro-variation` — structural perfection kept alive by subtle differences
- `layered pattern density` — multiple geometric systems overlapping without confusion
- `material preciousness as offering` — gold, lapis, polished stone as spiritual dedication
- `script as visual architecture` — calligraphy that structures space like a building

## Anti-Tokens

- `figurative representation`
- `empty negative space`
- `rough spontaneous mark`
- `asymmetric composition`
- `muted earth tones only`
- `narrative scene painting`

## Application Rules

1. Geometric pattern must feel infinite — the edge of the frame should suggest continuation, not termination
2. Calligraphy carries emotional weight through rhythm and proportion, not through pictorial illustration
3. Horror vacui is not clutter but devotional completeness — every surface filled is an offering of attention
4. Symmetry must be perfect at the structural level but alive at the detail level — micro-variations prevent mechanical deadness
5. Material preciousness (gold, lapis, polished stone) is not luxury display but spiritual dedication made visible

## Evaluation Criteria

- Does the geometric pattern suggest infinity beyond the frame?
- Does the calligraphy carry emotion through rhythm alone, without illustration?
- Does the filled surface feel devotionally complete rather than cluttered?
- Is symmetry structurally perfect but alive in its details?
- Does material preciousness feel like offering rather than display?
