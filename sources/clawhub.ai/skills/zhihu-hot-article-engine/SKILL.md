# Zhihu Hot Article Engine

Write Zhihu articles that rank on the hot list and earn genuine upvotes.

## When to use

- Writing a comprehensive Zhihu answer that needs to stand out among hundreds
- Creating a long-form Zhihu article (专栏文章) targeting a trending topic
- Analyzing a Zhihu question to determine whether it's worth answering
- Optimizing an existing answer that isn't getting traction
- Writing a hot-list strategy: timing, keyword density, and engagement baiting (legitimate)

## Core Principles

### 1. Zhihu Writing is Different

Zhihu is NOT a blog, NOT a social media post. It's a **credibility-first knowledge platform**:

| Aspect | Blog | Twitter | Zhihu |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Reader mindset | Skimming for info | Scanning for entertainment | Seeking **authoritative, structured answers** |
| Required depth | Medium | Very low | **Very high** |
| Personal story | Optional | Common | **Structure anchor** |
| Data/citations | Nice to have | Never | **Essential** |
| Update cycle | Once | Once | **Living document** (edit/sticky) |
| Length sweet spot | 1500-2500 words | 280 chars | **3000-8000+ words** |
| Credibility signal | Domain | Followers | **Credentials + data + experience** |

### 2. The Zhihu Algorithm

Zhihu's recommendation system ranks answers based on these factors (ordered by importance):

1. **Upvote count + velocity** — fast early upvotes = massive amplification
2. **Answer length** — longer answers (>3000 chars) consistently rank better
3. **Creator credibility** — verified credentials, past high-quality answers
4. **Dwell time** — how long readers stay on your answer (scroll depth)
5. **Engagement** — comments, collections, shares (collections = strong signal)
6. **Relevance** — how well your answer matches the question's topic cluster
7. **Freshness** — new answers get a temporary boost for first 48 hours

**Key insight**: The first 2 hours determine whether your answer goes viral or dies.

## Phase 0: Knowledge Reconnaissance

BEFORE writing, you MUST complete this research phase. Each item is mandatory.

### Step 1: Question Analysis
Scan the question:
- Is it a **hot question**? (check Zhihu hot list relevance)
- Is it **well-formulated**? (clear, specific, debatable)
- What's the **question type**?
  - 事实类 ("What is X?" ) → data-heavy, authoritative sources
  - 观点类 ("Do you think X?") → personal experience + reasoning
  - 经验类 ("How to do X?") → step-by-step, credibility via results
  - 评价类 ("How do you evaluate X?") → balanced, multi-perspective
- Who are the **top answerers**? (read 3-5 top answers to understand the landscape)

### Step 2: Knowledge Mapping
Search for:
- **Core concepts**: definitions, principles, academic references
- **Data points**: statistics, studies, surveys with specific numbers
- **Case studies**: real-world examples, historical analogues
- **Expert opinions**: what credible figures have said on this topic

### Step 3: Counter-Intuition Hunt
MUST find at least one counter-intuitive point:
- "Commonly believed X, but actually Y"
- "Most people think A, but research shows B"
- "The mainstream advice is wrong because..."

### Step 4: Citation Harvesting
Collect minimum 3 high-credibility citations:
- Academic papers (preferred)
- Official data/statistics
- Authoritative media reports
- Expert quotes with named attribution

### Step 5: Angle Selection
Choose your UNIQUE angle:
- [ ] **Contrarian angle**: Challenge the mainstream view
- [ ] **Expert insider**: Reveal what people don't know from the inside
- [ ] **Systematizer**: Provide a framework/step-by-step no one else has
- [ ] **Deep-diver**: Go 10x deeper than existing answers
- [ ] **Connector**: Connect two seemingly unrelated fields

## Phase 1: Structure Building

After Phase 0, build the answer structure:


## Phase 2: Writing Execution

### Writing Rules for Zhihu

| Rule | Explanation |
|:---|:---|
| **Direct answer first** | Never bury the lead. First 3 lines must answer the question |
| **分段≤5句** | Short paragraphs for mobile readability |
| **加粗关键句** | Bold the thesis statement in each section |
| **数据必须有来源** | Every number needs a citation |
| **每300字一个"钩子"** | A surprising fact, a question, or a provocative statement |
| **专业但不学院** | Use plain language, but show depth |
| **例子≥1个** | Every abstract point needs a concrete example |
| **字数3000+** | Short answers don't rank. Aim for 3000-8000 chars |
| **不要纯讲道理** | Mix personal story with data |
| **留白** | Not every point needs exhaustive explanation. Let readers fill gaps |

### Title Rules

For **专栏文章** (not answers), the title is critical:

- **含关键词**: Include the topic's core search keyword
- **数字 + 结果**: "3个方法"，"10年经验"，"5步做到"
- **悬念/冲突**: "为什么专业的人都不说真话？"
- **具体而非抽象**: "我如何从零学会XX" vs "学习XX的方法"
- **情绪触发**: 好奇、紧迫、认同、优越
- **A/B测试**: Write 3-5 title variants and pick the best

### Engagement Optimization

- **Asking questions**: End sections with a rhetorical question to boost comment engagement
- **Comments bait**: "有不同意见欢迎评论区讨论"
- **Collection candy**: "建议先收藏，慢慢看" (at a logical mid-point)
- **Upvote ask**: Subtle, at the end, with justification ("如果对你有帮助")

## Advanced Tips

### Hot-List Timing
- **Post timing**: Weekday evenings 19:00-22:00 (maximum active users)
- **Weekend**: Saturday 10:00-14:00 (information-seeking mode)
- **Avoid**: Late night (00:00-06:00) and working hours (09:00-12:00)
- **Boost window**: First 2 hours after posting — engage with every comment

### Building Credibility on a New Account
- Answer 3-5 smaller questions FIRST to build answer history
- Get your first 100 upvotes on easy questions before tackling big ones
- Credentials: Verify your real identity (知乎认证) if applicable
- Consistency: Answer 1-2 questions per week minimum

### Handling Controversial Topics
- Acknowledge opposing views: "很多人可能觉得……"
- Present data, not opinion: "根据研究显示……"
- Stay calm: never use aggressive language
- "我可能不对，但目前的理解是……"
- Invite counter-arguments: "欢迎补充或反驳"
