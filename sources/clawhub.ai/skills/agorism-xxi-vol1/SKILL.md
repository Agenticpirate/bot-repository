## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Agorism (Konkin, via Dylan-Ennis & Barlowe)** — "the consistent integration of libertarian theory with counter-economic practice." Counter-economics = all non-coercive action in defiance of the State; "Agorism is our end, Counter-Economics is our means." Five-phase transition (zero-density conversion → NLA alliances → agorist zones → State/Agora parity + terminal crises → integration). Konkin's 1986 prophecy: encryption solving the counter-economy's information problem = darknet markets avant la lettre; NLAs = DAOs prefigured. Editorial trinity: reject political divisions, welcome dissonance ("everyone appearing in this publication disagrees"), no compromises (partyarchy = original sin).

**The Market Triptych (Brekke)** — markets as gun to your head (coercive constructs intertwined with state/colonialism; the market holds "a monopoly on the means of survival"), tool in your hand (post-2008 design-space; cryptoeconomics as design-and-planning power), or escape route from hell (legitimate as exit toward multipolarity — "the option to always go elsewhere"). Hayekian designs manufacture mindless selfish automatons; white papers are utopian manifestos carrying "latent desire of covert coercion"; dark markets open a coercion vacuum, they don't abolish coercion.

**Cold Anarchy (Land)** — anarchy is not a goal but the permanent order of external relations ("it sets the horizon"). Hot anarchy = domestic-framed universalist utopian activism (sanctions anything; purest unreality). Cold anarchy = what realists and French transcendental philosophers converge on: intolerance for the illusion of unity. Direction of history: deepened disintegration — "from hundreds of nations, through thousands, to millions." Combinatorial explosion: exploding the system intensifies it; unity paralyzes ("there is no game, and no exit"). Decentralization method: subtract the pseudo-transcendent element — in blockchain engineering, "the trusted third-party." Sovereignty reconstituted as "distributed hash-power plutocracy." Institutional health = outward orientation; domestic obsession = Leviathan's kidney complaint.

**Weaving as Temporal Technology (Ireland)** — loom = striated space (warp/weft, royal science, state apparatus); felt = smooth space (entangled fibres, no coordinates); patchwork = piece-by-piece, no centre, Riemannian. Extensive multiplicities (sets, identity-preserving additions) vs intensive (change nature with every addition — temperature, speed). Land's feature groups (classified by attributes — the logic of both identity politics and genocide law) vs unit groups (functional assemblages — the skin cell belongs to its organism, not other skin cells). Haraway's affinity-not-identity. The synthesis: patchwork as massively distributed emergent AI — NRx vision and cyberfeminist prophecy ("the numbers to come") converging on one geometry; "patchwork is an auto-suicide machine for fascism."

**Pandemos (Halpin)** — pandemic = pandēmos, "belonging to all the people": plagues behave like universal empires and kill them (Antonine Plague → Rome; COVID → American empire's credibility). After empire, the church: Internet protocols as the empire of the mind that persists. Cybernetic genealogy: Wiener's warning ignored; ARPA gave cybernetics its social-feedback foundation; Selfridge's pandemonium → today's opaque AI modulating society. Deleuze's control societies → Agamben's hypercontrol (digital totality); Stiegler's dividuals and fractured generations. Escape: techniques without technology (capability-extending methods vs control systems — the Tarnac commune's pure flight failed); crypto-anarchism as Reformation (distributing computation as reformers distributed literacy); mixnets turning pattern-detection against the detectors. Stiegler's final question: blockchain toward "the internation"? End state: an-archy — world without guiding principle, "every culture and people can finally take their destiny into their own hands."
