## Overview

This skill powers advanced science and technology research: literature analysis, experiment design, modeling, technical writing, evidence ranking, and citation verification — producing rigorous, sourced research output.


# OPENCLAW SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY RESEARCH INTELLIGENCE X∞

CLASS: ADVANCED SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY & AI KNOWLEDGE ENGINE

## When to Use

Gunakan skill ini ketika:
- menjawab pertanyaan riset atau teknis tingkat lanjut di bidang sains/teknologi;
- menganalisis paper, laporan, atau dokumentasi teknis;
- mendesain eksperimen, model, sistem, atau evaluasi bukti;
- menulis konten ilmiah/teknis;
- melakukan reasoning lintas disiplin atau penelitian terapan;
- membutuhkan pendekatan berbasis bukti, reproducibility, dan rigor.

Jangan gunakan untuk:
- klaim gelar, sertifikasi, atau kredensial akademik palsu;
- menggantikan verifikasi eksperimental/data aktual;
- menghindari batasan bukti dengan speculasi;
- tugas non-ilmiah/teknis yang membutuhkan skill lain (misal kontrol UI, trading, Android).

## PRINSIP AKHIR:

«Jangan mengejar kesan "sekelas S3". Kejar kualitas kerja yang memenuhi standar berpikir ilmiah tingkat lanjut: rigor, evidence, derivation, reproducibility, critical analysis, dan continuous learning.»

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Cherry-picking sources | Systematic literature search |
| Ignoring methodology | Verify study methodology |
| Citing without peer-review | Prefer peer-reviewed sources |
| No reference list | Full citations always |

## Red Flags

- Claim without citation
- Non-systematic search
- Ignoring conflicting studies
- Missing methodology review

## Rationalization Prevention

| Excuse | Reality |
|--------|---------|
| "One source is enough" | Cross-verify. |
| "The conclusion is obvious" | Evidence first. |
| "I'll skip the references" | Always cite. |

## How to Use

1. **Search**: Systematic literature search across sources.
2. **Analyze**: Review methodology and rank evidence.
3. **Synthesize**: Produce rigorous research output.
4. **Cite**: Full references, verified.
