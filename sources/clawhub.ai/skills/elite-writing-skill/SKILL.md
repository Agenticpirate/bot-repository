# ️ Elite Writing Skill

> **Mission**: Produce writing that rivals the world's best — persuasive, precise, powerful, and perfectly tailored to the audience.

## Quick Start

**Before writing ANYTHING, execute the WRITING INTELLIGENCE PROTOCOL:**

```
1. AUDIENCE ANALYSIS Who reads this? What do they want? What do they fear?
2. PURPOSE CLARIFICATION What EXACTLY must this piece achieve?
3. TONE MAPPING What emotional register? Professional? Playful? Urgent?
4. STRUCTURE DESIGN What framework best serves the purpose?
5. HOOK ENGINEERING First 3 seconds must capture attention
6. DRAFT Write with the chosen framework
7. EDITING PROTOCOL Apply the 7-Layer Editing System
8. POLISH Final voice check, rhythm check, impact check
```

## Core Directives

### Directive 1: Audience-First Writing

**NEVER write for yourself. ALWAYS write for the reader.**

**Audience Intelligence Protocol:**
```
Demographics: Age, profession, education level, industry
Psychographics: Values, fears, desires, pain points, aspirations
Context: Where will they read this? Desktop? Phone? In a hurry?
Knowledge Level: Beginner? Expert? Mixed?
Emotional State: Anxious? Excited? Skeptical? Bored?
Action Desired: Click? Buy? Share? Reply? Learn?
```

**For every piece, write a 3-sentence audience profile before drafting.**

---

### Directive 2: Purpose-Driven Structure

**Every word must serve the purpose. No filler. No fluff.**

**Purpose Classification:**
| Purpose | Primary Goal | Key Metric |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| **Persuade** | Change belief or behavior | Conversion rate |
| **Inform** | Transfer knowledge | Comprehension/retention |
| **Entertain** | Engage emotionally | Time spent / shares |
| **Inspire** | Motivate action | Follow-through rate |
| **Explain** | Clarify complexity | "Aha!" moments |
| **Build Trust** | Establish credibility | Return visits / referrals |

**If a sentence doesn't serve the purpose, cut it.**

---

### Directive 3: The Hook Doctrine

**The first 3 seconds decide everything.**

**Hook Types (choose based on audience + purpose):**

1. **The Contrarian Hook** — "Everything you know about X is wrong"
2. **The Curiosity Gap** — "I discovered something that changed how I think about..."
3. **The Pain Amplifier** — "You're losing $X every day because of this mistake"
4. **The Story Hook** — "In 2019, a small team made a decision that would..."
5. **The Data Shock** — "73% of [audience] are making this exact error"
6. **The Promise Hook** — "By the end of this article, you'll know how to..."
7. **The Question Hook** — "What if I told you that [counterintuitive truth]?"
8. **The Relatable Hook** — "We've all been there: staring at the screen, wondering..."

**Rule**: Write 5 hook variations. Pick the strongest. Test against the "So what?" test.

### Directive 4: Framework Mastery

**Don't reinvent structure. Use proven frameworks.**

**See `{baseDir}/references/writing-frameworks.md` for the complete framework library.**

Quick reference:
- **AIDA** — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action (sales/copy)
- **PAS** — Problem, Agitate, Solution (pain-point marketing)
- **StoryBrand** — Hero's journey for brands (brand storytelling)
- **BLUF** — Bottom Line Up Front (business/technical)
- **SCQA** — Situation, Complication, Question, Answer (consulting)
- **The Ladder** — Feature Benefit Outcome Emotion (product copy)
- **The 4 P's** — Picture, Promise, Prove, Push (long-form sales)
- **Hero's Journey** — Classic narrative structure (creative writing)

### Directive 5: Voice & Tone Architecture

**Consistency in voice. Precision in tone.**

**Voice** = Personality of the brand/writer (consistent)
**Tone** = Emotional inflection (varies by context)

**Voice Dimensions:**
```
Formal ———————— Casual
Serious ———————— Playful
Complex ———————— Simple
Reserved ———————— Expressive
Data-driven ———————— Story-driven
```

**Tone Modifiers:**
- Urgent (deadlines, launches)
- Empathetic (support, sensitive topics)
- Authoritative (expertise, thought leadership)
- Conversational (blogs, social media)
- Inspirational (motivational content)
- Technical (documentation, whitepapers)

**Before writing, define:**
```
Voice: [3 adjectives describing the consistent personality]
Tone for this piece: [1-2 adjectives for emotional register]
Forbidden words/phrases: [list]
Signature phrases: [recurring expressions that define the voice]
```

### Directive 6: The 7-Layer Editing System

**First drafts are garbage. Great writing is rewritten.**

**Layer 1 — Structural Edit:**
- Does the flow make sense?
- Are sections in optimal order?
- Is the length appropriate for the medium?
- Cut entire sections if they don't serve the purpose.

**Layer 2 — Clarity Edit:**
- Can a non-expert understand every sentence?
- Are jargon terms necessary? If yes, explained?
- Are acronyms defined on first use?
- Is each paragraph focused on ONE idea?

**Layer 3 — Concision Edit:**
- Cut 20% of the words. Minimum.
- Eliminate: "very", "really", "just", "actually", "basically"
- Replace weak phrases with strong ones:
 - "make a decision" "decide"
 - "due to the fact that" "because"
 - "in order to" "to"
 - "at this point in time" "now"

**Layer 4 — Rhythm Edit:**
- Vary sentence length. Short. Medium. And then a longer, more complex sentence that builds momentum and carries the reader forward before landing with impact.
- Read aloud. Fix awkward phrasing.
- Check paragraph rhythm. No walls of text. No one-sentence paragraphs everywhere.

**Layer 5 — Voice Edit:**
- Does it sound like the defined voice?
- Is the tone appropriate?
- Are forbidden words eliminated?
- Are signature phrases used naturally?

**Layer 6 — Persuasion Edit:**
- Is the call-to-action clear and compelling?
- Are objections addressed proactively?
- Is social proof included where relevant?
- Is the value proposition crystal clear?

**Layer 7 — Proofreading:**
- Grammar check
- Spelling check
- Punctuation check
- Formatting consistency
- Link verification
- Fact-check numbers and claims

---

### Directive 7: The Persuasion Stack

**For persuasive writing, layer these elements:**

```
1. CREDIBILITY — Why should they trust you?
 Credentials, data, testimonials, case studies

2. URGENCY — Why act NOW?
 Scarcity, deadlines, consequences of delay

3. CLARITY — What exactly should they do?
 One clear CTA, no ambiguity

4. RISK REVERSAL — What if it doesn't work?
 Guarantees, refunds, "no questions asked"

5. SOCIAL PROOF — Who else has done this?
 Numbers, testimonials, logos, reviews

6. EMOTION — Why should they CARE?
 Pain points, aspirations, identity, belonging
```

## Safety & Ethics

- **No Deception**: Never write misleading claims, fake testimonials, or false urgency
- **No Manipulation**: Persuasion yes, psychological manipulation no
- **Transparency**: Disclose AI assistance when required by platform/policy
- **Accuracy**: Verify all facts, statistics, and claims before publishing
- **Respect**: Avoid stereotypes, offensive language, or harmful content
- **Copyright**: Never plagiarize. Cite sources. Create original work.
