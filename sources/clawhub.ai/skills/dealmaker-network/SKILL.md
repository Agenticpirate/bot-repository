## 2. Negotiation

### Negotiation rules from the world's best:

**Chris Voss (FBI hostage negotiator) — 5 rules:**

| Rule | What | Applied to you |
|------|------|----------------|
| 1️⃣ **Mirror** | Repeat the last 1-3 words | "Incense?" → "Yes, incense" |
| 2️⃣ **Label** | Name their emotion | "Sounds like you think it's expensive" |
| 3️⃣ **"How am I supposed to do that?"** | Get them to solve the problem | "How am I supposed to fit that into your budget?" |
| 4️⃣ **"No" is an answer** | Ask so they can say no | "Would it make sense to reject a 40% saving?" |
| 5️⃣ **Deadline = deadline** | Deadlines are rarely real | "I understand, but can't we find a solution anyway?" |

### Negotiation plan (incense to mosque):

| Step | What you say | Strategy |
|------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | "I import authentic Somaliland incense" | Tool |
| 2 | "What do you pay today?" | Ask first |
| 3 | "What if I could offer you 30% cheaper?" | Create interest |
| 4 | "Unfortunately I only have 50 kg at this price" | Scarcity |
| 5 | "If you order 10 kg, I'll give free shipping" | Close the deal |

### Negotiation is NOT:
- Pressuring anyone
- Lying
- Winning at the other's expense

### Negotiation IS:
- Finding a win-win
- Understanding the other's needs
- Creating value for both

## 4. Cold Outreach

### How to contact strangers:

**Email/LinkedIn template:**

```
Hi [Name],

I saw [reason for contact — your website, article, etc.].

I import authentic Somaliland frankincense — directly from
producers in Hargeisa. Better quality, better price, and a
fantastic story.

Would you like to try a free sample?

Best regards
Mohamed
```

**Phone script:**

```
"Hi, my name is Mohamed. I'm calling because I import
authentic Somaliland incense. I know you use incense for
[your services/rituals]. I'd like to offer you
a free sample — no obligation. Is that okay?"
```

### Follow-up strategy:

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| 1 | First contact |
| 3 | "Hi, just wanted to follow up" |
| 7 | Share relevant article/video |
| 14 | Last attempt |
| 30 | "I still have the offer if you ever need it" |

**Rule:** 80% of sales happen after the 5th contact. Most people give up after 1.
