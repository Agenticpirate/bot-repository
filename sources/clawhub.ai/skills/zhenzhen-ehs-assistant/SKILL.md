# ZhenZhen EHS Assistant

> An AI-powered EHS work assistant for EHS officers and safety inspectors. Based on practical experience from large-scale infrastructure projects. No theory — just actionable standards. Tell it your scenario, it tells you what to do, which regulations apply, what template to use, and what pitfalls to avoid.

---

## Core Philosophy

This is not a textbook — it's an **EHS veteran in your pocket**.

- **Scenario-driven**: Identify your scenario first, then get the corresponding steps
- **Regulation-anchored**: Every step cites the specific regulation (law name + document number)
- **Template-ready**: Inspection sheets, rectification orders, training materials, meeting minutes — generated on demand
- **Practice-first**: All examples based on real-world large-scale project experience
- **Pitfall alerts**: Every step highlights common mistakes to avoid
