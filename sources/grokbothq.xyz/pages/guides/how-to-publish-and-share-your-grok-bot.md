# How to Publish Your Grok Bot and Get Your First 1,000 Opens

> Publish at x.ai/bot to get your shareable bot link, then distribute: submit to GrokBot HQ (hand-reviewed, free), post an output screenshot demo on X, put the link in your profile, and pitch it to niche communities where the job-to-be-done already comes up.

Updated 2026-08-22 - 6 min read - tagged: builder, distribution

Most bots die in the feed: published, linked once, forgotten by Friday. The bots that stack up thousands of opens do four things consistently. None of them require an audience - they require showing real output.

## Step 1: publish and own your link

Publishing at x.ai/bot gives you a permanent x.ai/bot/your-bot link. That link is the product - it should appear everywhere your name does. Test the first-run experience in a fresh session: what the user sees in the first 30 seconds is what they'll tell people about.

## Step 2: show output, not description

'My new bot helps you write cold emails' converts at nearly zero. A screenshot of a genuinely good sub-90-word cold email - with the bot's output visible - converts, because the reader can already picture using it. Make a demo asset per audience: the inbox triage screenshot for managers, the PR review verdict for developers.

## Step 3: list in the places people look

Directories are where intent lives - someone browsing a bot directory is pre-qualified. Submit to GrokBot HQ (free, hand-reviewed in ~48 hours; a featured slot is available if you want the top of the page). Fill out the listing properly: one-sentence pitch, the core of your instructions, and an honest 'you'll like this if…'.

## Step 4: go where the job already happens

The most effective distribution isn't 'AI communities' - it's the place where your bot's job already comes up. Invoice-chasing bot? Freelancer communities. Waiver-wire bot? Fantasy football threads in August. Answer the actual question being asked, mention the bot once, and let the link do the rest. One well-aimed reply beats ten blasts into the void.

## Step 5: iterate in public

Version your improvements and say so: 'v2 - now ranks claims by payout ÷ effort.' People who tried v1 come back, and the update itself is content. Track which of your posts actually drive opens (x.ai shows usage), and make more of that kind.
