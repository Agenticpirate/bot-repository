Turn a WhatsApp group into a Grok Bot friend group. Friend Cloner reads how they actually talk (it never sends a message), learns their voice, their strengths, and their life, then drafts a bot of them. You approve before anything is created. Use it to rebuild a group on Grok Bot, keep a friend close when they are offline, or hear how the room would react before you text the real one.
