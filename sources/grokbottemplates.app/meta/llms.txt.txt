## How to install a template

Templates are installed in the Grok Bot app, not via this site.

1. Browse https://grokbottemplates.app/ or open a detail page at https://grokbottemplates.app/t/{slug}.
2. Tap **Add to Grok Bot** on Mac, Windows, or iOS.
3. Or open the `addHref` deep link (shape: `grokbot://app/v1/bot-template?id=<shareId>`).

Each template includes `slug`, `name`, `byline`, `summary`, `tags`, `shareId`, `shareUrl`, `addHref`, optional `sourceUrl`, and `updated` in the catalog JSON and on its HTML detail page.

## What is shared

Share links include identity, description, skills, and routines. They do **not** include computer access, logins, history, or memory.

## Privacy

Share URLs are public. Never put secrets, API keys, or private credentials in a bot you intend to share.
