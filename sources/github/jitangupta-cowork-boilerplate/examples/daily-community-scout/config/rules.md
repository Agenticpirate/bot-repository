# Rules — Community Scout

## Quality Filter

### SKIP these posts:
1. Link dumps with no substance (just a link with < 2 sentences of commentary)
2. Promotional launch announcements with no original experience
3. Reshares/reposts with generic reactions ("Great read!", "This is huge")
4. Hype posts with no depth ("Claude Code is amazing!" — no specifics)
5. Engagement bait ("What's your favorite AI tool? Comment below!")
6. Job postings (unless they reveal interesting production usage)
7. Posts older than 48 hours with < 5 engagements

### KEEP these posts:
1. Genuine experience reports with specifics
2. Thoughtful criticism or praise with evidence
3. Posts with screenshots showing real work
4. Active discussions with 10+ substantive replies
5. Comparison posts with actual testing
6. Questions from practitioners

## Engagement Styles (rotate across these)

**[Experience] (max 30-40%)** — Share what you learned from specific experience.
**[Curious] (30-40%)** — Ask genuine questions about THEIR experience.
**[Tip] (20-30%)** — Share a specific technique or trick. Pure value.
**[Validate] (for negative posts)** — Acknowledge pain first. Don't rush to solutions.

## Banned Patterns
- Fanboy language ("Game-changer!", "Mind-blown!")
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Leading with credentials when a question would be more natural
- Trashing competitors
- Self-promotion without adding value
