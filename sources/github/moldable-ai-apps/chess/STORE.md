Make your next move with Moldable or Stockfish.

Play chess against Moldable in chat, continue hands-free with Voice, or choose
**Stockfish 18** for a local chess-engine opponent. Practice guided positions,
turn on the tutor, and replay past games to see how a position developed.

- Play as White or Black at friendly, club, or master difficulty.
- Choose Moldable, the optional Stockfish engine, or a simple local opponent.
- Play with another person on the same board.
- Explore guided lessons, hints, and your saved game history.
- Continue on your paired iPhone with the same workspace and game state.

## Optional Stockfish engine

Stockfish installs on demand: a one-time download of about 16 MB, then local
play on your desktop. It uses the pinned Lichess `@lichess-org/stockfish-web`
0.4.2 build of Stockfish 18 smallnet. No Stockfish binary or personal game
history is bundled in the app release.
