Your money, made clear.

Money brings your accounts, spending, net worth, debts, and investments into customizable dashboards. Start with a dashboard that matches what you care about, explore the demo, and connect your own accounts when you are ready.

- Track balances, cash flow, and spending categories.
- Explore transactions and the numbers behind each card.
- Shape dashboards and formula-backed cards through Moldable.
- Keep your imported financial data in your local Moldable workspace.

## Connecting accounts

Bank connections require your own **Plaid Production Client ID and Secret**. Add `PLAID_CLIENT_ID` and `PLAID_SECRET` in **Moldable Settings → Vault**, then choose **Connect an account** in Money. The connection dialog links to the Plaid dashboard and explains setup when keys are missing. Bank authentication opens through Plaid in your browser.

Plaid credentials and account access tokens are held by aivault. The app release includes no connected accounts or personal financial history; its demo data is illustrative.
