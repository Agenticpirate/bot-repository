Save on X. Find it here.

Bookmarks turns your saved X posts into a private, searchable local library. Find a post by its text, author, or link, browse folders, and open saved posts with their media.

- Import bookmarks from your own X account.
- Search your local archive and browse selected bookmark folders.
- Refresh your library from inside Moldable.
- Read saved posts and present them in conversation.

## Connecting X

Sign in to X in your browser, then open Bookmarks and choose the signed-in browser profile you want to use. The app asks you to confirm the connection before syncing. An advanced OAuth connection option is also available for users with their own X client ID.

Your archive and connection state belong to your Moldable workspace. The app release includes no saved posts, browser sessions, or personal account data.
