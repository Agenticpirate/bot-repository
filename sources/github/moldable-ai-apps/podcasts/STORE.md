# Turn your curiosity into a podcast

Pick a topic, choose a length, and create a narrated episode to listen to on your Mac or iPhone. Podcasts writes a script, generates narration and cover art, and keeps your episodes in a searchable personal library.

- Create episodes from a topic, or ask Moldable to supply a researched script.
- Listen with playback speed controls, skip buttons, and a transcript.
- Follow generation progress, archive episodes, and resume saved work after an interrupted generation with Retry.
- Keep your listening progress in sync between desktop and iPhone.

Episodes are personal: this app does not publish a public feed or distribute your audio. In-app topic generation does not browse the web. Narration requires an OpenAI API key stored in aivault; API usage is billed separately from ChatGPT or Codex subscriptions.
