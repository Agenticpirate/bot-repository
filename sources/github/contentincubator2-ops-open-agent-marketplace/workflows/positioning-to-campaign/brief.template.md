# Positioning to Campaign Workflow

## Purpose

Turns market insight and product proof into a landing-page message, ad angles, and lifecycle email sequence.

## Required inputs

- market context
- target audience
- offer
- proof points
- constraints

## Success criteria

- The output names a target audience.
- The recommendation makes clear tradeoffs.
- The handoff is structured enough for the next agent or human to continue.
- The work avoids unsupported claims and private data.
