# Content Engine Workflow

## Purpose

Builds a repeatable content system from audience research to calendar and repurposed assets.

## Required inputs

- audience
- business goals
- core themes
- channels
- cadence

## Success criteria

- The output names a target audience.
- The recommendation makes clear tradeoffs.
- The handoff is structured enough for the next agent or human to continue.
- The work avoids unsupported claims and private data.
