# Social Listening Workflow

## Purpose

Turns public discussion patterns into insights, quote banks, and content angles.

## Required inputs

- topic
- public channels
- time range
- brand risk boundaries
- desired output

## Success criteria

- The output names a target audience.
- The recommendation makes clear tradeoffs.
- The handoff is structured enough for the next agent or human to continue.
- The work avoids unsupported claims and private data.
