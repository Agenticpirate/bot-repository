## 2026-08-12 — v0.1.0 day-0 catalog (0 → 219 listed; held 0)

First public list. Source: Grok Bot in-app marketplace export (Settings, Plugins) captured August 12, 2026 — the day after [Grok Bot launched](https://x.ai/news/introducing-grok-bot) as an early beta. 219 plugins across 13 catalog categories. Every listed URL is the plugin's public [Cursor Marketplace](https://cursor.com/marketplace) page, cross-checked against the live web catalog the same day.

Scope notes from the capture:

- Automations on the public marketplace are a separate product surface and are not in this list.
- Five public-marketplace plugins were *not* in the Grok Bot in-app roster and were left out: Figma, Merge, PagerDuty, Compound Engineering, Zenity.
- This catalog is not Grok Build (`xai-org/plugin-marketplace`) and not grok.com Connectors.

Snap Stack debut: the overnight sales outbound Bot (Apollo.io, Clay, Gong, HubSpot). Composed, not field-tested.
