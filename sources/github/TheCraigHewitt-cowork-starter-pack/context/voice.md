# Voice

Co-work reads this file every time it drafts something for you — emails, reports, summaries, anything written. This is where you get outputs that sound like you instead of like AI.

Two to three paragraphs and a few lists is enough. Be specific. Show examples.

---

## Example (a filled version — delete this when you fill in your own)

I write like I talk. Short sentences. Direct. I skip pleasantries in internal comms ("Hope you're doing well") and get to the point. External comms are warmer — I'll ask how someone's quarter is going, but I still won't pad.

My structure for any written update: what happened, what it means, what I need from you. In that order. No lead-ins like "I wanted to reach out to let you know that..." — just the fact.

I use tables and bullet lists when the information is parallel (three options, four metrics, five questions). I use prose when I'm explaining a decision or making an argument.

### Words and phrases I use
- "Here's the thing"
- "The short version is"
- "Let me be direct"
- "What I'm seeing is"
- "Worth noting"

### Words and phrases I never use
- "Crushing it" / "killing it"
- "Game-changer" / "10x"
- "Synergy" / "leverage" (as a verb)
- "I hope this finds you well"
- "Just wanted to circle back"
- Emojis in anything going to anyone outside my direct team

### Tone by context
- **Internal Slack:** fast, lowercase, no greeting, emojis OK
- **Internal email:** direct, short paragraphs, proper punctuation
- **External email (clients, vendors):** warm opener, direct middle, clear ask at the end
- **Reports / written summaries:** factual, tables where useful, no qualifiers unless there's real uncertainty

## Customization notes

- **If you write in someone else's voice** (EA drafting for a principal, marketer drafting for a founder, ghostwriter) — fill this file with *their* voice, not yours. Put your personal voice in `preferences.md` if you need to separate them.
- **If you're a team** and your brand voice is shared — build this from your style guide. Paste your actual brand voice doc here if you have one.
- **If you have no idea what your voice is** — paste 3–5 of your best-received recent emails or documents and let Co-work extract the patterns. Tell it: "This is the voice. Save what you learn to this file." That's the fastest way to bootstrap this.

## A sanity test

When this file is done, run this prompt in Co-work:

> Draft a short email to [someone] about [any recent topic]. Match my voice from `voice.md`. Before you send it to me, tell me what you did to match the voice.
