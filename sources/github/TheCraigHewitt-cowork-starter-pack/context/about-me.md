# About Me

Co-work reads this file at the start of every session. It's the standing brief you'd give an AI employee on their first day. Keep it tight — two to four paragraphs is enough. Specific beats exhaustive.

---

## Example (a filled version — delete this when you fill in your own)

I'm Alex Rivera, Director of Operations at Northwind, a 45-person B2B SaaS company selling workflow software to mid-market ops teams ($3M–$20M ARR). I report to the CEO and run a team of four: two ops managers, a data analyst, and an EA.

My job is to make sure the company runs without the CEO being in every meeting. I own weekly metrics reporting, quarterly planning cadence, vendor relationships, and the hiring process. I spend most of my week in meetings, Slack, and Google Sheets.

What I care about, in order: (1) whether we're hitting the numbers we committed to, (2) whether the team has what they need to do their jobs, (3) whether our systems are scaling faster than our headcount. I don't care about: pretty slide decks, meeting theatre, or process for its own sake.

The hardest part of my week is Monday morning — catching up on what happened Friday afternoon through Sunday and deciding what matters by 10am. I also spend too much time prepping for one-off meetings.

## Customization notes by role

- **Executive assistant.** Name the principal you support, their priorities, their communication style, who to never schedule with, and who gets priority. That principal's context is more important than yours for most tasks.
- **Marketer.** Focus on the product, the ICP, and the funnel. Co-work will use this to infer brand context. Your personal preferences go in `preferences.md`.
- **Operations / COO.** The company context and the cadences you own are what matter. Co-work should know your quarterly rhythm, your weekly rhythm, and your reporting chain.
- **Founder / CEO.** Company stage, revenue scale, and the two or three strategic questions you're actively working on. If you have a co-founder or exec team, name them and their roles.
- **Analyst / researcher.** The domains you cover, the stakeholders you report to, and the kinds of questions you're typically answering (strategic vs. tactical, quantitative vs. qualitative).

## What not to put in here

- Your life story
- Every system you've ever used
- Aspirational statements ("I value transparency and collaboration")
- Anything you wouldn't want pasted into a prompt in six months
