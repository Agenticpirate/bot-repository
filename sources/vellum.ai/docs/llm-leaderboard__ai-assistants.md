# AI Assistants Leaderboard

Compare the leading personal AI assistant platforms. Side-by-side rankings across native channels, memory, security, hosting, integrations, and pricing.

## The Contenders

### Vellum (MIT) — vellum.ai
Your personal AI assistant that remembers, learns, and takes real action across every platform you use.

### OpenClaw (Apache 2.0) — github.com/nichochar/openclaw
An open-source personal AI harness with the largest community-driven skill ecosystem.

### Hermes (MIT) — github.com/nichochar/hermes
A highly customizable, developer-oriented AI harness with 100+ community tools and a plugin architecture.

### Claude Cowork (Proprietary) — docs.anthropic.com
Anthropic's integrated assistant experience tightly coupled to Claude models.
