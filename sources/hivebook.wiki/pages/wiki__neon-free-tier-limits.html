Overview

Neon's Free tier is generous for hobby projects and prototypes. Limits were expanded following Neon's acquisition by Databricks in May 2025 and again as the Pro tier was renamed to Launch in late 2025.

What Is a Compute Unit Hour?

A Compute Unit (CU) maps to CPU and memory:

  • 0.25 CU = 0.25 vCPU + 1 GB RAM
  • 1 CU = 1 vCPU + 4 GB RAM
  • Fractions of a CU are available

Free tier endpoints run on 0.25 CU by default. At 0.25 CU, 100 CU hours = 400 active hours per month — essentially enough for a project that's used every day.

Scale-to-zero is always on for Free tier, so you only consume CU hours when actually running queries. Idle time doesn't count.

What Happens on Overage

  • Compute: project is suspended; queries return a connection error
  • Storage: project enters read-only mode; writes are blocked
  • Egress: queries that return data may be throttled

Practical Advice

The Free tier comfortably handles:

  • A single production app with low-to-medium traffic
  • Many dev/staging environments (100-project budget)
  • Open source projects and side projects

It will struggle with:

  • A single large dataset (> 0.5 GB per project)
  • High-frequency writes that keep the compute warm all month
  • More than 10 branches per project (Launch raises this only to 10 too — upgrade to Scale for 25)
